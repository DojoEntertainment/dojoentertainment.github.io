- **A table** (ping pong tables work best)
- **Plastic cups** (12 or more)
- **A small bouncy ball** (like a ping pong or Powerball-style ball)
- **Plenty of drinks**

## How to Play Powerball

### Setup

1. **Divide into two teams** (3–6 players per team works best).
2. **Arrange six cups** in a triangle at each end of the table (beer pong style).
3. **Fill the cups** with your drink of choice. (Beer, cocktails, or soft drinks for the designated drivers.)

### The Rules

1. **Game On:** Each team takes turns throwing or bouncing the ball, aiming to land it in the opposing team’s cups.
2. **Drink Up:** When a ball lands in your cup, your team must drink the contents.
3. **Dare Power:** If you make a cup, your team can challenge the other to a dare (think: NSFW dares or meme drinking games for bonus laughs). Use cards from Drinking Dojo or make up your own!
4. **Elimination:** Once a cup is hit, it’s removed. First team to clear all of the opponent’s cups wins.
5. **Bonus Round:** Want to spice it up? Add a custom rule for the final cup—like a viral drinking game challenge or a wild house party idea from Drinking Dojo.
