Concerts are all about letting loose, bonding with friends, and making memories. Add a drinking game to the mix, and you’ve got the recipe for an epic night. ## The Ultimate Concert Drinking Game

### What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (be responsible, always)
- A group of friends who love music and a good laugh
- A phone or notepad (for keeping track of dares and rules)
- Optional: Custom dare cards or meme drinking games from [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com)

### How To Play

**Step 1: Set Up Your Drinking Crew**  
Gather your friends before the show, at the venue, or during the afterparty. Decide if you want to play with everyone or split into teams for added chaos.

**Step 2: Choose Your Rules**  
Pick your favorite set of drinking game rules, or build your own with our custom drinking game builder. Here’s a starter pack to get you going:

- **Take a sip if:**
  - The band plays their biggest hit
  - Someone in your group tries (and fails) to start a chant
  - You spot a legendary concert outfit in the crowd

- **Finish your drink if:**
  - The lead singer jumps into the crowd
  - The encore is a total surprise

- **Dare challenges:**  
  - Snap a selfie with a stranger (show proof or drink)
  - Start a dance-off in your row
  - Try to catch a drumstick or guitar pick—if you fail, chug

- **Meme drinking games:**  
  - If someone gets caught on the jumbotron, everyone drinks
  - If a meme-worthy moment happens (think wild costumes or on-stage fails), nominate the best meme-maker in your group to come up with a dare

**Step 3: Keep It Wild, Not Reckless**  
Concert drinking games are all about fun—know your limits, respect the venue, and look out for your mates. Stay hydrated and don’t drink and drive!
