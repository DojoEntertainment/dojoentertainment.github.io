### What You Need

- A TV (or stream) showing the latest NFL games and power rankings
- A group of friends (4-10 is perfect)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, mocktails—it all counts)
- Printed or digital copy of the current NFL power rankings
- Dare cards or a custom drinking game builder (check out [Drinking Dojo’s custom tools](https://drinkingdojo.com))
- Optional: meme cards for extra spice, penalty flags for rule-breakers

### How to Play: NFL Power Rankings Drinking Game

Get ready to turn every highlight, fumble, and hot take into an unforgettable drinking challenge. Here’s how to host your own nfl power rankings drinking game like a pro:

#### 1. Draft Your Teams

- Each player picks (or is assigned) one or more NFL teams from the current power rankings.
- For extra fun, randomize picks or draw teams from a hat. The more rivalry, the better.

#### 2. Set the Rules

Pick your favorite rules from below or use a custom drinking game builder to create your own:

- **Touchdown Toast:** Whenever your team scores a touchdown, everyone else drinks.
- **Turnover Trouble:** If your team turns the ball over (interception, fumble), you take a dare challenge or finish your drink.
- **Ranking Rumble:** If your team moves up in the nfl power rankings during the broadcast, assign a drink to any player you choose. If they drop, you drink twice.
- **Wild Card:** Draw an NSFW dare card or meme drinking game card after every big play or viral moment.
- **Penalty Chugs:** If your team gets a penalty, chug for 5 seconds.
- **Upset Alert:** If the lowest-ranked team beats the highest-ranked team, everyone finishes their drink.

#### 3. Add Some Dare Challenges

Spice things up with dare challenges between quarters or during commercial breaks. Use [Drinking Dojo’s dare packs](https://drinkingdojo.com) or make your own. Think: dance-offs, meme reenactments, or even Fortnite drinking game rounds for the truly bold.

#### 4. Keep It Going All Season

- Update the teams as the power rankings shift week-to-week.
- Try themed rounds for special events (like an election day drinking game or wedding party games if you’re celebrating more than just football).

### Pro Tips for Epic NFL Drinking Nights

- Make sure everyone drinks responsibly (hydrate and know your limits!).
- Mix in some non-alcoholic rounds for designated drivers and friends who want to join the fun without the buzz.
- Use penalty flags for rule-breakers—winner gets to assign a dare!
