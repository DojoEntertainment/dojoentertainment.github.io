## Top Black Friday Party Drinking Games

### 1. Shop or Shot

**Materials Needed:**  
- Deck of cards  
- Drinks for everyone  
- List of fake Black Friday deals (get creative!)

**How to Play:**  
1. Everyone sits in a circle and picks a card.  
2. The dealer reads out a "Black Friday deal" — some are real, some are totally made up.  
3. Guess if the deal is legit or fake. Get it wrong? Take a shot. Get it right? Give out a shot to someone else.  
4. Wild dare challenge: Draw a Joker, and you must do an NSFW dare chosen by the group!

**Perfect For:** Breaking the ice and testing your deal-spotting skills (or your ability to bluff).

### 2. Meme Mayhem

**Materials Needed:**  
- Phone or laptop  
- Access to viral memes (try a meme drinking game app or just Google the latest memes)  
- Drinks

**How to Play:**  
1. Each round, one player shares a meme with the group.  
2. Everyone votes on whether it’s “Black Friday Relatable” or not.  
3. If your meme flops, you drink. If it’s a hit, assign drinks!  
4. Bonus: Use our custom drinking game builder to add your own meme-inspired rules.

**Perfect For:** Social media junkies and groups who love a good laugh.

### 3. Dare Roulette

**Materials Needed:**  
- Dare challenge cards (you can DIY or use Drinking Dojo’s packs)  
- Drinks

**How to Play:**  
1. Spin a bottle or use a random name picker to choose the first challenger.  
2. Draw a dare card — anything from “Call out your worst Black Friday impulse buy” to “Attempt your best Fortnite dance.”  
3. Complete the dare or take a drink.  
4. Keep it rolling with wild NSFW dares for the fearless!

**Perfect For:** Parties that need a blast of energy (and a few epic stories).

### 4. Couples Cash-In

**Materials Needed:**  
- Drinks  
- List of “shopping scenarios” (e.g., you both want the last TV, you spot your ex at the store)

**How to Play:**  
1. Each couple picks a scenario and acts it out.  
2. The rest of the group decides who wins the “deal.”  
3. Losing couple drinks. Winning couple assigns drinks.  
4. For extra spice, toss in some wedding party games or election day drinking game twists!

**Perfect For:** Double dates, couples’ nights, or wedding after-parties.
