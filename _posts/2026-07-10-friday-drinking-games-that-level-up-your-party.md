## 1. Meme Mayhem

**Get ready for: Laughter, viral moments, and a little friendly humiliation.**

**What you need:**  
- A deck of meme cards (or load up your favorite meme generator online)  
- Drinks for everyone  
- A sense of humor

**How to play:**  
1. Each player draws a meme card or finds a viral meme on their phone.  
2. The group votes on who in the room the meme matches best.  
3. That person takes a drink and has to create a dare challenge inspired by the meme. (Example: If the meme is about bad dancing, the dare could be “do your worst TikTok dance or drink twice!”)

*Why it rules*: This meme drinking game keeps the energy high and the drinks flowing. Plus, it’s perfect for sharing hilarious moments on social media.

## 2. Dare or Drink Challenge

**Best for: Groups who love wild dares and have little shame.**

**What you need:**  
- A set of written dares (keep them NSFW or tame, your call)  
- Drinks for everyone

**How to play:**  
1. Place all dares in a bowl or hat.  
2. Go around the circle. Each player draws a dare.  
3. Complete the dare or take a drink instead.  
4. Bonus: For every dare completed, the group adds a new custom dare to the bowl—making things crazier as the night goes on.

*Pro tip*: Use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to whip up a set of epic dares in seconds.

## 3. Fortnite Friday (The Party Drinking Game Edition)

**For the gamers and the competitive spirits.**

**What you need:**  
- Fortnite (on your console/PC) or watch a stream  
- Drinks for everyone

**How to play:**  
1. Every time you get eliminated, you drink.  
2. If you snag a Victory Royale, everyone else drinks.  
3. Add in viral drinking games rules: for every “epic fail” moment, the group comes up with a dare challenge for the unlucky player.

*Why it’s iconic*: It turns your gaming session into a full-blown party, and even non-gamers will want in on the fun.

## 4. Couples Clash

**Perfect for: Date night with a twist, or couples at a group hang.**

**What you need:**  
- Drinks for both partners  
- A list of couples drinking games challenges (think: “Who said I love you first?”, “Who’s more likely to plan a surprise?”, etc.)

**How to play:**  
1. Ask each couple a challenge question.  
2. If they disagree, both drink.  
3. If they agree, everyone else drinks.  
4. Add bonus dare challenges for the couples who get the most wrong answers.

*Why play*: It’s hilarious, revealing, and a great icebreaker for double dates or group nights.
