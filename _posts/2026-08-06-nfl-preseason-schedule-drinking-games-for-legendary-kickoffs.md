## How To Play: NFL Preseason Drinking Game Rules

**Classic Play-Along**

1. **Draft Your Players**  
   Everyone picks a player or team to “root” for (even if it’s just for the memes).

2. **Take A Sip When:**
   - Your player gets subbed in or out
   - The announcers mention “preseason” or “rookie”
   - Someone in the room yells at the TV (bonus points for creativity)
   - There’s a commercial for fantasy football

3. **Finish Your Drink If:**
   - A kicker misses an extra point
   - There’s a wild meme moment on social (bonus: share it with the group)
   - Someone attempts an NFL-worthy dance and nails it (or absolutely flops)

4. **Dare Challenges:**  
   Every quarter, pull a dare card from your Drinking Dojo pack. These can be:
   - **NSFW dares** (if your squad’s down for it)
   - NFL meme challenges (impersonate your favorite commentator)
   - Viral drinking games twists (e.g., drink with your non-dominant hand until halftime)
