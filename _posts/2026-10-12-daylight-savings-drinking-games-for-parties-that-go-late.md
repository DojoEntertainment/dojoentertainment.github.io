## Daylight Savings Switch Up — The Ultimate Drinking Game

### What You Need:
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, seltzers—dealer’s choice)
- A clock or phone with a timer
- A deck of cards (optional, for bonus rounds)
- A group of friends ready for a good time

### How To Play:

1. **Set The Timer:**  
   Every 10 minutes, the timer goes off. When it does, everyone takes a drink. Simple start.

2. **Spring Forward Challenges:**  
   Whenever someone says “What time is it?” or checks their phone for the time, they have to do a dare challenge picked by the group. (Pro tip: Use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create some spicy NSFW dares if your crew can handle it.)

3. **Fall Back Flip:**  
   If you catch someone changing the clocks or talking about losing/gaining an hour, they have to take two drinks or complete a meme drinking game dare.

4. **Wildcard — The Hour Change:**  
   At the moment the clocks actually change (or when you decide it’s “midnight” at your party), everyone draws a card. Each suit means:
   - **Hearts:** Choose someone to drink with you
   - **Clubs:** Make a house rule for the next 15 minutes
   - **Diamonds:** Start a “Never Have I Ever” round
   - **Spades:** Dare the person on your left

5. **Last Call Showdown:**  
   When the party’s winding down, play a quick-fire round of “Truth or Dare” using only daylight savings-related prompts—like “What’s the weirdest thing you’ve ever done because you forgot about the time change?” or “Dare: Post a meme about daylight savings to your story.”
