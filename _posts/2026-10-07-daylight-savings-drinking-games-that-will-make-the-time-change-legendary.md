## Daylight Savings Drinking Game Rules

Here’s a crowd-pleaser designed just for the time change, mixing classic drinking game vibes with custom dares and meme-worthy moments. No two rounds are ever the same—especially with the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) ready to shake things up.

### You’ll Need:
- At least 3 players (the more, the messier)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cider, cocktails—whatever fits your vibe)
- A phone or clock visible to all
- A deck of cards or the Drinking Dojo app for wild dares and meme drinking games
- Optional: A timer

### How to Play

**1. The Clock Is Your Boss**
- Every time the clock changes to a new hour, everyone takes a drink. If you’re playing during the time change, double up when the hour “repeats” (hello, 2 AM twice).

**2. Time Traveler Challenges**
- At the start of every round (set a 10 or 15-minute timer), draw a dare from your deck or generate one using Drinking Dojo’s custom dare challenge builder. Anyone who skips a dare must finish their drink.

**3. “Spring Forward” or “Fall Back”**
- If it’s spring: Every time someone forgets the new time, they do an NSFW dare or take two sips.
- If it’s autumn: When the clock goes back, everyone does a group dare—think singing your favorite meme song, or a viral TikTok dance.

**4. Bonus: The Sleepyhead Rule**
- First person to yawn (no fake yawns!) has to do a dare drawn by the group or take a big gulp.

**5. Meme Drinking Game Twist**
- Assign a meme to each player (think “Distracted Boyfriend,” “Woman Yelling at Cat,” etc.). If someone acts out their meme during the game, they get to make a rule for the next round.
