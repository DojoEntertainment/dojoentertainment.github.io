### Classic Guy Fawkes Party Drinking Game Rules

#### **How to Play**

1. **Set the Scene:** Gather everyone in a circle, drinks in hand.
2. **Deal the Cards:** Each player draws a card from the deck. Each number or face card represents a different dare or rule (see below).
3. **Draw and Drink:** Whoever draws must complete the corresponding challenge. If they refuse, they drink instead.

#### **Card Meanings**

- **Ace:** “Remember, remember the fifth of November…” Recite the full rhyme or take a drink.
- **King:** Dare challenge! The group comes up with a wild dare (NSFW dares optional, keep it within your crew’s comfort zone).
- **Queen:** Meme time—show your best Guy Fawkes meme or improv one. Fail and drink.
- **Jack:** Election day drinking game twist—guess a famous historical fact about Guy Fawkes or the UK. Wrong answer? Drink.
- **Numbers 2–10:** Each number is sips to take. Simple, but effective.

### Bonfire Bingo: A Unique House Party Idea and Wedding Party Game

Perfect for outdoor house party ideas and wedding party games, Bonfire Bingo mixes classic drinking challenges with Guy Fawkes Day traditions:

1. **Create a Bingo card** with squares for things like:
   - Someone mentions fireworks
   - Someone says “gunpowder”
   - Someone sings “God Save the King”
   - Someone quotes V for Vendetta
2. **First to complete a line** gives out drinks to anyone they choose.
