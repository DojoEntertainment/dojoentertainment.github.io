## How to Play the MLB Draft Drinking Game

Ready to spice up the selection show? Here’s how to bring the Drinking Dojo vibe to your MLB draft night.

### Basic Rules

1. **Take a Sip When:**  
   - Your team is on the clock.  
   - A new player’s highlight reel is shown.  
   - An analyst predicts a trade that doesn’t happen.  

2. **Take Two Sips If:**  
   - A draftee hugs everyone at their table.  
   - The commissioner botches a name.  
   - There’s a meme-worthy moment on Twitter (bonus if you spot it first).  

3. **Finish Your Drink If:**  
   - Your team makes a shocking pick.  
   - Someone at your party admits they don’t know any players drafted.  
   - A player thanks their pet in their acceptance speech.  

### Dare Challenges and NSFW Dares

Want to take it up a notch? Add dare challenges every time your group can’t agree on a rule call. The loser has to:  
- Post a meme about the draft on their story.  
- Attempt their best commentator impression.  
- Do a (PG or NSFW) dare from your Drinking Dojo pack.  

*Always keep it fun and consensual—know your limits!*
