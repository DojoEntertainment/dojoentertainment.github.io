## Classic World Cup Drinking Game Rules

Ready to kick things off? Here’s how to play our go-to World Cup 2026 drinking game:

### Basic Game Setup

1. **Split Into Teams:** Root for opposing countries, or randomize teams if you’re feeling spicy.
2. **Choose Your Drink:** Everyone picks their poison.
3. **Set the Dare Level:** Decide if you want to keep it chill or crank up the NSFW dares for maximum chaos.

### Core Drinking Rules

- **Goal Scored:** Everyone supporting the conceding team takes a drink. If it’s an own goal, double it.
- **Yellow Card:** All players drink (because someone’s always playing dirty).
- **Red Card:** Shot time for the player supporting the carded team.
- **Missed Penalty:** Everyone drinks, but the person backing the kicker takes two.
- **Commentator Cliché:** Whenever the commentator says “back of the net,” “dream start,” or “end to end,” everyone drinks.
- **VAR Review:** Dare challenge time! Pick a dare from your Drinking Dojo pack or custom builder.
- **Half-Time:** Meme drinking games round—everyone shares their best meme (winner assigns drinks).

### Bonus Dare Challenges

- **Hat Trick Hero:** If a player scores three, last person to shout “Hat Trick!” does a dare.
- **Epic Save:** If the keeper pulls off a wild save, everyone must do a funny accent for the next play; worst accent drinks.
