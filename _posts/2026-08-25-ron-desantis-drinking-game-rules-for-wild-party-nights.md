## How to Play: Ron DeSantis Drinking Game

1. **Set Up the Stream**  
   Put on a Ron DeSantis press conference, interview, meme montage, or political debate. The more viral, the better.

2. **Choose Your Poison**  
   Pick your drink. If you’re feeling bold, mix in some themed cocktails (Floridian Rum Punch, anyone?).

3. **Game Rules**  
   Take a sip, chug, or complete a dare every time the following happens:

   - **He says “Florida”** – Everyone drinks.
   - **Mentions “freedom” or “woke”** – Last person to raise a fist drinks.
   - **Awkward silence or weird facial expression** – Dare challenge: Draw a meme face on yourself with a marker.
   - **Mentions another politician (Biden, Trump, etc.)** – Next player picks someone to take two drinks.
   - **Viral meme moment** – Group selfie with your best shocked face, then drink.
   - **If someone shouts at the TV** – That person does an NSFW dare.
   - **Bonus: If he references Disney, Fortnite, or anything pop culture** – Waterfall! Everyone drinks until the person to their right stops.

4. **Custom Dares Bonus Round**
