## Indiana Fever Drinking Game Rules

Ready to vibe with your squad? Here’s how to play:

### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Players:** Everyone grabs a seat and a drink. Bonus points for Fever jerseys or team colors.
2. **Set Your Drink Levels:** Decide if you’re sipping, chugging, or taking shots for each challenge.
3. **Game On:** Start the Indiana Fever game and keep these rules handy.

### Take a Sip When

- The Fever score a three-pointer
- A player argues with a ref
- The commentator mentions Caitlin Clark
- There’s a timeout
- The camera catches a wild fan in the stands

### Take Two Sips If

- The Fever hit a buzzer-beater
- There’s a meme-worthy play or celebration
- The opposing coach calls back-to-back timeouts

### Dare Challenges

Feeling bold? Mix in some **dare challenges** for the ultimate party drinking game:

- **NSFW dare:** Loser of the quarter’s trivia round has to post a wild meme or do a funny TikTok dance.
- **Election day drinking game** twist: Guess the next Fever scorer—get it wrong, do a dare.
- **Couples drinking games** bonus: Pair up and swap drinks anytime there’s a technical foul.

### Finish Your Drink If

- The Fever win with a double-digit lead
- A player fouls out in the final quarter
- The game goes to overtime
