## How To Play: NFL Scores Yesterday Drinking Game

**What You’ll Need:**
- The list of NFL scores from yesterday (grab ‘em on your phone)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, seltzers — whatever fuels your party)
- Cups for everyone
- A way to stream or replay highlights (optional but highly recommended)
- Your game face

### Basic Rules

1. **Kickoff Cheers**  
   Everyone chooses a team. When your team scores, you take a celebratory sip. If your team lost, you start the night with a “consolation shot.”

2. **Touchdown Turn-Up**  
   Every time a touchdown is mentioned in the scores, everyone downs a drink. If it’s a game-winning touchdown, make it a double.

3. **Meme Play of the Day**  
   Did a play go viral or spark a meme? The group votes for the best meme-worthy moment from yesterday’s games. The person who finds the funniest meme gets to assign a dare challenge.

4. **Interception Interruption**  
   Whenever you see an interception in the scores, the person to your left must swap drinks with you. If you’re feeling savage, add an NSFW dare to the mix for extra spice.

5. **Wildcard Dares**

### Bonus Round: Party Drinking Game Add-Ons

- **Couples Drinking Games**  
  Playing with your partner? Every time your rival teams play, the losing side does a dare.
- **Wedding Party Games**  
  NFL fans at a wedding? Set up a quickfire version at the reception — perfect for breaking the ice.
- **House Party Ideas**
