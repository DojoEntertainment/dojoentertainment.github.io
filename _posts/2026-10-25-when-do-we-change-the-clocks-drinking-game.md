## The When Do We Change The Clocks Drinking Game

### What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, or mocktails—no judgment)  
- A deck of standard playing cards  
- A phone or device with a clock (bonus if you have a wall clock to play with)  
- A bunch of friends ready for epic dare challenges  

### How To Play

**Set Up:**  
Gather your group in the living room, kitchen, or wherever the party’s at. Make sure everyone has a drink and can see the time.

#### The Rules

1. **Countdown Hour:**  
   - When the clock hits the hour DST changes (usually 2:00 am for most regions), everyone must shout “Time Warp!”  
   - Last one to shout drinks.

2. **Card Time Challenge:**  
   - Draw a card. Each suit means a different drinking dare:  
      - **Hearts:** Share a meme or funny story about forgetting to change the clocks. If you can’t, drink.  
      - **Diamonds:** Make up a new rule for the group—break it, and you drink.  
      - **Spades:** Dare challenge! Choose someone to do an NSFW dare (keep it safe, folks).  
      - **Clubs:** Drink for the number on the card (face cards = 3 sips).

3. **Time Traveler’s Choice:**  
   - Every time someone checks their phone for the real time, they have to reveal the time and make a toast. Everyone drinks.

4. **Lost Hour Dare:**  
   - If it’s spring forward, everyone must give up their drink for one round and do a silly challenge instead (think: do your best Fortnite dance).  
   - If it’s fall back, everyone gets a free pass to skip a dare of their choice—once.
