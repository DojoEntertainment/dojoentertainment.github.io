## How To Play NBA Schedule Drinking Games

### 1. Pick Your Game Night

Choose a night with a juicy matchup. Rivalries, close standings, or superstar showdowns? The wilder, the better.

### 2. Set Up Your Rules

Here are some Drinking Dojo-approved rules to get started. Mix and match, or use our custom drinking game builder to create your own!

#### Basic Rules

- **Tip-Off Toast**: Everyone drinks at the start of each quarter.
- **Three-Point Splash**: Drink every time your chosen team hits a three-pointer.
- **Airball Alert**: Last person to shout “Airball!” when it happens takes a dare.
- **Timeout Trouble**: During every timeout, draw a dare from the pile—think meme drinking games or NSFW dares for the bold.
- **Foul Play**: Whenever your team commits a foul, finish your drink if it’s a technical; sip if it’s anything else.

#### Dare Challenges

- If a player dunks over someone, the group votes on the best meme reaction—loser does a dare.
- Missed free throws mean everyone has to say a player’s name—last one drinks.
- At halftime, everyone must complete a wedding party game-style challenge (sing a team chant, do a TikTok dance, or invent a Fortnite drinking game move).

#### Bonus Rounds

- **Overtime Mayhem**: Every overtime starts with everyone finishing their drinks.
- **Buzzer Beater**: If the game ends with a buzzer-beater, everyone completes a double dare challenge.

## House Party Ideas With an NBA Twist

- Create team-themed cocktails or shots based on who’s playing.
- Use our custom drinking game builder to add local flavor—think Toronto Raptors dares for Canada, or a Sydney Kings wild card for Australia.
- Add couples drinking games rules for double dates during the playoffs.

## Tips To Keep It Social and Wild

- Rotate who picks the next dare or game rule each quarter.
- Snap and share your best meme drinking games moments—bonus points for viral drinking game-worthy clips.
- Keep the dares spicy—but know your limits! The best party drinking games are fun and safe for everyone.
