## How to Play Tennis Scores Drinking Game

### Setting Up

1. **Split into teams or go head-to-head:** Singles (1v1) or Doubles (2v2+).
2. **Decide on your drink:** Everyone pours their drink of choice.
3. **Designate a scorekeeper:** Someone (sober-ish) to track the scores.

### The Rules

- **Serving:** Flip a coin to decide who “serves” first. The server starts the round by asking a dare or challenge to the opposing player/team.
- **Rally:** If the other side completes the dare, they win the point. If they fail or refuse, the serving team wins the point *and* the losing player/team drinks.
- **Scoring:** Use regular tennis scores—15, 30, 40, game. First to win four points takes the game.
- **Deuce Drama:** If both sides get to 40-40, it’s deuce! Alternate dares until one side wins two points in a row.
- **Change Sides:** After each game, switch up who’s serving.
