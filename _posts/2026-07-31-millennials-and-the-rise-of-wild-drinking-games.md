## Top Trending Drinking Games for Millennials

Ready to level up your next pregame or party? Here are some millennial-favorite drinking games that guarantee unforgettable memories:

### 1. Meme Drinking Game

**Materials:**  
- Index cards or phone app  
- Drinks of choice  
- A wicked sense of humor

**How to Play:**  
- Each player writes or finds their favorite meme and shares it with the group.  
- Everyone votes for the best meme each round.  
- Losers take a drink, and the winner gives out a dare challenge to anyone at the table.

_Pro Tip: Up the ante with some NSFW dares, if your group is game!_

### 2. Dare Roulette

**Materials:**  
- Deck of dare cards (or use [Drinking Dojo’s custom builder](https://drinkingdojo.com))  
- Drinks

**How to Play:**  
- Players draw a card and must complete the dare or take a drink.  
- Get creative with your dares—think “text your ex” or “do your best Fortnite dance.”  
- The more outrageous, the better.

### 3. Couples Drinking Games

Perfect for double dates or close-knit crews, these games bring out the laughs and friendly competition.

**Example Game: Truth or Drink Couples Edition**

- One couple asks another a question. If they refuse to answer, they drink.  
- Throw in some spicy NSFW dares to keep things interesting!

### 4. Fortnite Drinking Game

Have a gamer in the group? Try the ultimate Fortnite drinking game:

**How to Play:**  
- Every time you get knocked out, take a sip.  
- If you win a battle royale, choose someone to finish their drink.  
- Bonus: Loser must do a dare challenge or meme impression.

### 5. Wedding Party Games

Millennials love personalizing their events—especially weddings. Try a custom game:

- Set up a “newlywed dare challenge” where guests submit dares for the couple.  
- The couple must complete the dare or assign drinks to the crowd.  
- Works for destination weddings from Copenhagen to California!
