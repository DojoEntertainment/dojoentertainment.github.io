- A weather app or live tropical storm forecast (or even just a YouTube weather report)  
- Your favorite drinks (cocktails, beer, or whatever’s on hand)  
- A group of adventurous friends (2+ players)  
- Dare cards, slips of paper, or our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to make your own rules and dares  

### How to Play

1. **Tune In**  
   Pick a tropical storm weather forecast from your favorite source—weather app, YouTube, or TV.

2. **Assign Rules**  
   Before the forecast starts, agree on your set of drinking game rules. Here’s a tried-and-true set to kick things off:

   - **Every time you hear “tropical,”** take a sip.  
   - **If the forecast mentions wind speeds over 50 mph (or 80 kph),** do a dare from the dare deck.  
   - **Whenever someone says “storm surge” or “flooding,”** the last person to shout “Cheers!” drinks twice.  
   - **If the meteorologist says “stay inside,”** everyone must do a group toast and take a drink.  
   - **If a city in your country is mentioned,** the person closest to that city invents a meme drinking game rule for the round.  
   - **Spot a weather meme or funny blooper?** Everyone drinks.

3. **Custom Dare Challenges**  
   Spice it up with NSFW dares (if everyone’s game) or let the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) suggest wild party tasks. Think: “Imitate the meteorologist for 30 seconds,” or “Invent a new weather term and make everyone guess what it means.”

4. **Ride Out the Storm**  
   Play along for the length of the weather report, or keep the game going as long as the drinks (and dares) are flowing. Add in extra challenges if you want to mix things up—like a Fortnite drinking game round or a “wedding party games” twist if you’re celebrating something special.

### Pro Tips for Maximum Mayhem

- Switch up the rules if the forecast gets repetitive—house party ideas are all about creativity.  
- Add a couples drinking games mini-round: whenever the word “warning” pops up, couples must swap drinks.  
- For big groups, split into teams and compete in drinking challenges (who can craft the best weather meme on the spot?).  
- Always drink responsibly and know your limits—mayhem is great, but safety comes first.
