## How To Play: NFL Power Rankings Week 5 Drinking Game

### Step 1: Set Up Your Teams

- Split into small groups or play as individuals.
- Each person (or team) picks an NFL team from the week 5 power rankings.

### Step 2: Review the Rankings

- Pull up the official NFL power rankings for week 5 on your device.
- Read out the rankings for everyone.

### Step 3: Game On—Drink To The Rankings

Here’s where the party drinking games magic happens:

**Every time your chosen team is mentioned in a hot take, meme, or ranking commentary:**
- Take one sip.

**If your team moved up in the rankings:**
- Give out two drinks to anyone at the table.

**If your team dropped spots:**
- Take a dare challenge from the Drinking Dojo deck.

**If your team is ranked in the top 5:**
- Start a “cheers” round—everyone drinks!

**If your team is in the bottom 5:**
- You must complete an NSFW dare or finish your drink (your choice and your dignity).

**Bonus:**
- Whenever someone passionately defends their team, everyone else takes a sip.
- If there’s a viral meme about a team, that team’s picker gives out a drink.
