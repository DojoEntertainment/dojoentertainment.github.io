## How to Play Playstation Plus Monthly Games Drinking Challenge

This party drinking game is all about creating house party ideas that blend the excitement of new game drops with classic drinking challenges. Here’s how you get started:

### 1. **Pick Your Game**

Choose one of the latest Playstation Plus monthly games everyone’s excited about. Action, shooter, battle royale (looking at you, Fortnite fans), or chaotic co-op—anything goes.

### 2. **Set Up Your Dare Challenges**

Before you play, create a list of **dare challenges** or use a custom drinking game builder to make things spicy. For example:

- **First to die in-game:** Take a shot.
- **Epic fail (falling off a map, accidental team kill, etc):** Draw a meme card and do the dare.
- **Win a match or complete a mission:** Everyone else drinks.
- **Unlock a trophy or achievement:** Assign a drink to another player.
- **Rage quit:** Finish your drink and post a meme in your group chat.

Pro Tip: Use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to make your own rules, dares, and even NSFW dares if your crowd wants to go wild.

### 3. **Add Some Meme Drinking Games**

Mix in meme drinking games for extra laughs. For example, whenever a particular catchphrase or meme-worthy moment happens in-game (think “It’s high noon!” or “Victory Royale!”), everyone has to drink.

### 4. **Bonus Party Drinking Games**

If you’re waiting for your turn or just want to turn things up, try quick-fire wedding party games or couples drinking games between rounds. Think “Never Have I Ever: Gamer Edition” or “Truth or Dare: Controller Swap.”

## House Rules to Keep It Legendary

- **Know your limits.** Hydrate, eat snacks, and look out for each other.
- **Keep it fun.** No one should feel pressured to drink more than they want.
- **Make it inclusive.** Got friends who don’t drink? Swap drinks for dares or meme challenges.
