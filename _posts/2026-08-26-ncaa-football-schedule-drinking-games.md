## How to Play: NCAA Football Schedule Drinking Game

### Basic Rules

Gather your friends, tune into any NCAA football game, and let the drinking games begin. Here’s how it works:

1. **Pregame Picks:**  
   Before kickoff, everyone picks their team. If you’re split, even better—more chances for drinkable drama.

2. **Game Play:**  
   Drink based on what happens in the game. Try these classic prompts, or make your own with a custom drinking game builder:
   - **First Down:** Everyone sips.
   - **Touchdown:** Losing team drinks. Winning team cheers and picks someone for a dare challenge.
   - **Interception or Fumble:** Shot for the team whose offense messed up.
   - **Field Goal Missed:** Everyone drinks for the kicker’s pain.
   - **Flag on the Play:** Whoever’s wearing the most team colors drinks.
   - **TV Timeout:** Quickfire meme drinking games—share the funniest football meme in the group chat, loser drinks.

3. **Halftime Dare Challenges:**  
   At halftime, toss in some wild NSFW dares or wedding party games if your crew’s got guests from every walk of life. Use Drinking Dojo’s dare generator or get creative. Think: “Do your best mascot dance,” or “Send a cringe football pun to your ex.”

4. **Endgame:**  
   Final whistle means everyone on the losing team finishes their drink. Bonus: Victory lap for the winning team—pick anyone in the room for a couples drinking game or house party idea from the Drinking Dojo packs.
