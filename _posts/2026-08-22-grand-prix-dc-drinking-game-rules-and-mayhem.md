## How to Play Grand Prix DC

### Setting Up

1. **Create Your Track:** Lay out a “track” using cups, cards, or even sticky notes—each spot is a checkpoint with a challenge or dare.
2. **Assign Drivers:** Each player picks their “car” (can be a toy, coin, or just themselves) and lines up at the starting line.
3. **Deal the Cards:** If you’re using cards, each one represents a different dare or challenge (see below for ideas). If using the Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder, generate your digital race pack!

### Game Play

1. **Start Your Engines:** Players take turns rolling a die or drawing cards to move along the track.
2. **Hit a Checkpoint:** Land on a spot? Draw a card or complete the dare/challenge listed for that checkpoint. 
    - **Dare Challenges:** Chug, truth-or-dare, meme reenactments, or NSFW dares for the bold.
    - **Drinking Challenges:** Waterfall, “never have I ever,” or classic viral drinking games like flip cup.
3. **Pit Stops:** Special spots mean group challenges—think Fortnite drinking game rounds, or even a quick “election day drinking game” trivia for bonus moves.
4. **Winner’s Circle:** First to cross the finish line wins, but the real prize? Legendary party rep and maybe some embarrassing memes for tomorrow.

### Sample Dares and Challenges

- **Speed Chug:** Finish your drink before the next player’s turn.
- **Meme Drinking Game:** The group nominates the player who best recreates a viral meme (loser drinks).
- **Wedding Crashers:** Best fake wedding toast—winner gives out drinks.
- **Couples Pit Stop:** If couples are playing, they must do a dare together.
- **NSFW Dare:** Only for the brave (and consenting)—think truth-or-dare with a spicy twist.
