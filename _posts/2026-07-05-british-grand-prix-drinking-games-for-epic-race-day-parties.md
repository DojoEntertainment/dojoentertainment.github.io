## The Ultimate British Grand Prix Drinking Game

Here’s how to play:

### **Setup**

- Gather your squad and split into teams if you want to up the competition.
- Everyone picks a driver or a team to back for the race (draw names if you’re feeling spicy).

### **Rules**

Drink every time:

- Your chosen driver gets overtaken  
- There’s a crash, spin-out, or safety car  
- The commentators mention the “Silverstone crowd”  
- Someone complains about the British weather  
- A pit stop takes less than 3 seconds  
- The camera shows a celebrity in the crowd  
- Someone says “box, box, box”  
- Lewis Hamilton or Lando Norris gets mentioned (double if you’re playing in the UK)

**Bonus Dare Challenges:**  
- If your driver gets a penalty, pull a dare from the meme drinking games jar (or use the custom drinking game builder for NSFW dares if your group’s up for it).
- If there’s a red flag, everyone has to create their own mini drinking challenge—winner gets bragging rights and the next round poured for them.

### **Victory Lap**

At the end of the race, the person whose driver placed highest gets to make up a wild dare for another player. Make it unforgettable—think wedding party games level of chaos.

## House Party Ideas to Max Out the Fun

- **Race-themed cocktails:** Create drinks like “Pit Stop Punch” or “Podium Prosecco.”
- **Couples drinking games twist:** Pair up and combine your dare challenges for double the laughs.
