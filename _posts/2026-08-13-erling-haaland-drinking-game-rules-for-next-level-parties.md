- A group of 3+ friends

## How To Play The Erling Haaland Drinking Game

1. **Pick Your Players:** Gather everyone in a circle, drinks in hand. Assign everyone a number or name for tracking dares.
2. **Set Up The Dares:** Choose a set of NSFW dares, meme dares, or classic party dares. Shuffle them in a pile or use an app.
3. **Match Time:** Start the game as soon as the football match kicks off. Get ready for chaos.

### The Rules

Every time one of these things happens during the match, the group drinks or does a challenge:

- **Haaland Scores:** Everyone does a shot. Last person to react does a dare challenge.
- **Haaland Celebrates Wildly (think meme-worthy):** Everyone makes their best Haaland face. Best impression picks someone to drink.
- **Haaland Misses a Big Chance:** Group vote—who jinxed him? That person finishes their drink.
- **Commentators Mention “Robot,” “Machine,” or “Viking”:** First person to shout “Drinking Dojo!” chooses who drinks.
- **Haaland Gets an Assist:** Two drinks for everyone. Bonus: Someone tells a Haaland meme.
- **Haaland Gets Subbed Off:** Everyone stands and toasts. Last to stand does a dare.
- **Game Ends With Haaland on the Pitch:** Finish your drink and share your wildest dare from the pile.

#### Bonus Dare Challenges

Add these for extra spice:

- **NSFW dares** for the brave hearts (think wedding party games or couples drinking games style).
- **Custom meme dares** themed around Haaland’s iconic moments—get creative!
- Optional: Add a “Fortnite drinking game” round at halftime for double the fun.
