## How to Play the Christian McCaffrey Drinking Game

This party drinking game is all about celebrating (and sometimes surviving) every epic McCaffrey moment on the field. Here’s how to get started:

### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Player:** Everyone watches Christian McCaffrey. If you’re feeling ambitious, assign friends to other key players for bonus action.
2. **Drink Triggers:** Take a sip, chug, or complete a dare whenever any of these things happen:
    - McCaffrey scores a touchdown (everyone finishes their drink)
    - He breaks a run for 20+ yards (2 big gulps)
    - The announcer says “versatile” or “dual threat” (take a sip)
    - McCaffrey catches a pass (everyone cheers, last to cheer drinks)
    - He celebrates with a meme-worthy dance (oldest player does a dare)
3. **Spicy Dare Challenges:** When McCaffrey gets a first down, draw a dare card. Think NSFW dares for the bold, or meme drinking games for the meme lords. Examples:
    - Text your ex “Christian McCaffrey for MVP” or finish your drink
    - Attempt your own touchdown celebration in the middle of the room
    - Make up a custom Fortnite drinking game rule for the next play

4. **Bonus Rounds:** If McCaffrey fumbles, everyone does a group shot. If he throws a pass, last person to yell “CMC!” does a dare.
