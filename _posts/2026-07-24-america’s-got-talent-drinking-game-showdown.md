## How to Play: The Rules

The goal is simple: sip, chug, or unleash wild dares whenever the show hits those classic AGT moments. Here’s how to play:

### The Basic Rules

**Take a Sip When:**  
- The judges disagree or hit their buzzers  
- An act gets a standing ovation  
- Someone mentions their “dream” or “journey”  
- There’s a dramatic camera zoom to the audience  

**Take Two Sips When:**  
- There’s a dog, child, or grandma on stage  
- Simon makes a sarcastic comment  
- Anyone gets emotional (tears count double!)  

**Finish Your Drink If:**  
- The golden buzzer gets slammed  
- An act completely fails or gets all buzzers  
- A judge joins the act on stage  

### Dare Challenges

Want to level up? Toss in some dare challenges for the losers each round. Didn’t guess who would make it through? Pull a dare card and get ready to embarrass yourself—think meme drinking games meets NSFW dares.

#### Dare Ideas:  
- Perform your own “talent” for the group  
- Send a cringe selfie to your group chat  
- Read a dramatic monologue in your worst accent  
- Do a TikTok dance on command
