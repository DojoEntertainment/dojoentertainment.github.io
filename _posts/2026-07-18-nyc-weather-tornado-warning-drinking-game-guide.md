- Your favorite drinks (keep it safe!)

- A phone for memes and dare challenges (bonus points for tornado memes)

### The Rules

We’re bending the rules tonight—NYC style. Here’s how to ride out the tornado warning with the most epic drinking games:

- **Take a Sip:**  
  Every time the news anchor says “rotation,” “cell,” or “shelter.”
- **Finish Your Drink:**  
  If you hear a siren, or the news cuts to street footage of wild NYC weather.
- **Dare Challenge:**  
  If the weather map shows Brooklyn or Queens, pick someone to do a dare from our viral dare challenges. NSFW dares for the brave, and meme dares for the meme lords.
- **Take Two Sips:**  
  For every tornado meme sent in the group chat.
- **Social Shot:**  
  If someone in your group says, “This would only happen in New York,” everyone takes a shot.  
- **Custom Rule:**
