### NFL Week 6 Drinking Game Rules

Mix and match these party drinking games and dare challenges for the ultimate NFL experience. Adapt any rules to fit your vibe—because, at Drinking Dojo, rules are made to be bent.

#### Basic Game: Touchdown Sips

- **Take 1 sip:** Every time your team gets a first down.
- **Take 2 sips:** When any team scores a field goal.
- **Take 3 sips:** For every interception or fumble.
- **Finish your drink:** If your team scores a defensive touchdown.

#### Dare Challenge: Red Zone Roulette

- Whenever a team enters the red zone (inside the 20-yard line), the group draws a dare card. The person who guesses the next play wrong must complete the dare. Bonus points for using our [custom dare creator](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add your own wild NSFW dares!

#### Viral Meme Drinking Game

- Print out memes or use your phones to pull up NFL memes.
- If a meme-worthy moment happens (cringe coach face, epic celebration, wild fan in the stands), everyone votes on the best meme match and the winner assigns drinking penalties.

#### Couples Drinking Game Twist

- Couples (or pairs) compete head-to-head. If your chosen player makes a big play, you get to assign drinks or a dare to another couple. Perfect for a double date or house party.

### Bonus: House Party Ideas for NFL Week 6

- Set up a “Penalty Box” for anyone who complains about the refs—must complete a dare or drink.
- Make a “Fantasy Fail” rule—if your fantasy player fumbles or gets injured, you have to do a shot.
- Wedding party games style: Create team-based challenges for your group, with the losing team facing a viral drinking game dare.

### Safety First
