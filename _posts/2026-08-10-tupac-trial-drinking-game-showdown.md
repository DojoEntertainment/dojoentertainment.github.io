## How To Play the Tupac Trial Drinking Game

Set the scene: everyone’s comfy, drinks in hand, Tupac trial content queued up. Here’s how it goes down:

### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Poison**  
   Choose your drink and get your cups ready. This is about fun, not chaos—pace yourself!

2. **Start the Stream**  
   Hit play on your favorite Tupac trial recap or doc. Bonus points for live chats, interviews, or reaction videos.

3. **Drink When You Hear or See:**
   - “Tupac” or “2Pac” is mentioned
   - Any mention of “Shakur,” “West Coast,” or “Biggie”
   - The phrase “unsolved case”
   - A reference to California or iconic Tupac lyrics
   - Any wild conspiracy theory

4. **Dare Challenges for Spicy Moments**
   - If someone shouts a famous Tupac line, draw an NSFW dare card and complete the challenge (think: embarrassing confessions, meme-worthy dance-offs, or wild wedding party games style dares).
   - When a dramatic plot twist is revealed, the last person to react must do a dare or finish their drink.

5. **Custom Drinking Challenges**
   - Spot a meme-worthy moment? Pause and let the group create a custom rule. (Example: “Every time someone says ‘alibi,’ everyone must swap drinks.”)
   - If someone can rap a verse from a Tupac song without messing up, hand out two drinks to anyone you choose.

6. **Couples and Teams**  
   Split into teams for some rounds—if your team misses a trivia question about the trial, everyone takes a drink (great for couples drinking games energy).

### Bonus: Fortnite or Election Day Twist

- **Fortnite Drinking Game Crossover:**  
  Between trial clips, switch to a quick Fortnite round. Every elimination equals a drink, and the first one out does a dare.

- **Election Day Drinking Game Vibe:**  
  Whenever a political figure is mentioned in the trial coverage, everyone raises a glass and drinks together.
