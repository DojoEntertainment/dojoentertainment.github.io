## Top Raleigh Weather Drinking Games

### 1. **Sun’s Out, Sips Out**

Perfect for: Rooftop bars, backyard hangs, lakeside chills

**What you need:**  
- A deck of cards  
- Your favorite drinks  
- Sunglasses for style (optional, but recommended)

**How to play:**  
- Draw a card. Odd = take a sip, Even = give a sip.  
- If the sun’s out, everyone to your left takes a sip.  
- If clouds roll in, everyone to your right takes a sip.  
- If it starts raining, finish your drink and shout “Weather Wizard!” Whoever says it last does a dare challenge.

### 2. **Raleigh Rain Roulette**

Perfect for: Cozy nights in, house parties, or when the weather can’t make up its mind

**What you need:**  
- A phone with a weather app  
- A custom drinking game builder (find one at [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com))

**How to play:**  
- Every 15 minutes, check the weather app.  
- If the temperature changes by more than 2ºC/5ºF, everyone drinks.  
- If there’s a weather alert, the group does a viral drinking game or an NSFW dare (keep it safe and fun).  
- Let the custom builder add wild, weather-themed dares to keep things spicy.

### 3. **Lightning Meme Mashup**

Perfect for: Meme lovers, game nights, anyone who loves a good laugh

**What you need:**  
- Phones with meme apps or a meme drinking game pack  
- Drinks  
- A sense of humor

**How to play:**  
- Each round, someone finds a weather meme (the crazier, the better).  
- Vote on the funniest one—loser drinks.  
- If thunder cracks while you’re playing, everyone does a group dare challenge, like acting out a weather forecast in their best TV anchor voice.
