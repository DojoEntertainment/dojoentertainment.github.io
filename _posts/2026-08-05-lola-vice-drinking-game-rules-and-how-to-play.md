- A group of 3 or more (the more, the wilder)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, seltzers—dealer’s choice)
- A deck of cards or a drinking game app (optional but fun)
- A dare list (customize it or grab one from Drinking Dojo)
- An open mind and a sense of humor

## How To Play Lola Vice

### 1. Set Up Your Dojo

Gather your friends in a circle. Make sure everyone’s drink is full—trust us, you’ll need it.

### 2. The Lola Vice Master

Pick someone to be the first “Lola Vice Master.” You can draw straws, pick the last person who took a shot, or just call out the loudest party animal.

### 3. The Vice Challenge

The Lola Vice Master draws a card or picks a challenge from the dare list. Examples could be:

- “Do your best wrestler entrance or drink twice.”
- “Imitate a viral meme or finish your drink.”
- “Challenge another player to a dance-off. Loser takes a shot.”

### 4. Accept or Drink

The chosen player must accept the dare or complete the challenge. If they refuse, they take a drink (or two, if the group’s feeling spicy).

### 5. Pass the Vice

After every round, the title of Lola Vice Master moves clockwise. New person, new challenge, more chaos.

## Pro Tips To Make It Even Wilder
