## How to Play: The Buckeye Bash Drinking Game

This isn’t your basic take-a-shot-when-they-score setup. Here’s how you make Ohio State football into the ultimate party drinking game:

### Setup

1. **Pick Your Game Pack:** Download or create a custom drinking game builder set with Ohio State football themes — think dares, meme challenges, and wild penalty rules.
2. **Divide Into Teams:** Buckeyes fans vs. “the other guys,” or just mix it up.
3. **Deal Out Dare Challenges:** Shuffle dare cards or use a digital dare generator. Mix in some NSFW dares if your crowd is up for it.

### Basic Rules

**Take a sip when:**
- Ohio State scores a touchdown
- The commentators mention “The Horseshoe”
- The marching band performs on screen
- There’s a meme-worthy coach reaction

**Finish your drink if:**
- There’s a pick-six or kickoff return TD
- Someone in the room yells “O-H!” and no one responds “I-O!”

**Dare Challenges:**
- Every turnover, pick a dare challenge card and complete it (think: silly dance, viral meme impression, or a spicy truth-or-dare)
- Losing team at halftime has to do a group dare (bonus points for creativity!)

**Penalty Flag:**
- If anyone gets caught checking their phone instead of watching a play, they get a penalty — draw a card from the NSFW dares pile or chug!

### Extra Points: Meme Drinking Games

Mix things up mid-game by adding in meme drinking games — whenever a classic Buckeye meme pops up (serious face paint, wild crowd signs, or a Brutus Buckeye sighting), everyone drinks. For the ultimate experience, let the custom drinking game builder create a meme challenge list before kickoff.
