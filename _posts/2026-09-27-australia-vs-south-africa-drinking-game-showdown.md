### What You’ll Need

- At least 4 players (split into Team Australia and Team South Africa)
- A deck of cards (or use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) for a digital experience)
- Drinks of choice (keep it local with Aussie beer or South African wine for bonus points)
- Paper and pen for scoring
- Dare challenges list (extra spice: throw in some NSFW dares or meme drinking games cards)

### How To Play

#### 1. **Form Your Teams**
Divide into two groups: Team Australia and Team South Africa. If you have more than eight people, make smaller squads and rotate in.

#### 2. **Set Up the Scoreboard**
Draw a scoreboard for both teams. Each round, teams can score points for winning challenges or lose points for failing dares.

#### 3. **Draw and Play**
Shuffle your deck of cards. Each turn, a player from each team draws a card and faces off in a challenge, dare, or drinking task.  
- **Red Cards:** Aussie-themed challenges (think: attempt your best Aussie accent, do a Tim Tam Slam, or answer trivia about Australia).  
- **Black Cards:** South Africa-themed dares (like: try a South African slang phrase, do a Springbok victory dance, or answer trivia about South Africa).

Optional: Use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to mix in viral drinking games or wild meme drinking games for extra chaos.

#### 4. **Dare Challenges**
If a player refuses a dare, their team drinks. If they nail it, the other team drinks. Want to spice it up? Add in NSFW dares or couple up for some couples drinking games action.

#### 5. **Drinking Challenges**
Every third round, throw in a classic drinking challenge:  
- Flip Cup  
- Kings Cup  
- Never Have I Ever (with a wedding party twist if you’re celebrating)

#### 6. **Winning the Showdown**
First team to 15 points wins ultimate bragging rights (and picks the next party playlist). Losers face a group dare—think viral, meme-worthy, and totally unforgettable.
