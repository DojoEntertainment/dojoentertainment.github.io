## Game 1: Meme Roulette

Bring on the viral drinking games energy. Meme Roulette is simple, hilarious, and perfect for Sunday nights that need a boost.

**How to Play:**

1. **Everyone brings their favorite meme (printed or on their phone).**
2. **Shuffle the memes and place them face down.**
3. **Take turns drawing a meme and showing the group.**
4. **Whoever laughs or reacts first takes a drink.**
5. **Bonus dare:** If no one laughs, the person who drew it has to complete an NSFW dare or a wild drinking challenge.

## Game 2: Dare Dominoes

Perfect for groups who love dare challenges and aren’t afraid to get a little wild.

**How to Play:**

1. **Stack a pile of pre-written dares (from Drinking Dojo or your own).**
2. **Players take turns drawing a dare and deciding to do it or pass.**
3. **If you pass, you drink—but if you do it, the person to your left must top your dare with something even crazier or drink twice.**
4. **Keep the chain going until someone backs down—then everyone cheers and drinks.**

## Pro Tips for Your Sunday Night Main Event

- **Pace yourself:** Sunday doesn’t have to mean a total write-off on Monday.
- **Stay hydrated:** Alternate water with drinks, especially with dare-heavy games.
- **Snap pics and vids:** Sunday memories are best when they’re meme-worthy.
- **Switch it up:** Try house party ideas each week—maybe one Sunday is all about NSFW dares, the next a wedding party game theme.
