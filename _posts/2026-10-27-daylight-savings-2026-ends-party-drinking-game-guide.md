## Why Daylight Savings Ending Deserves a Party

Let’s be real—getting an extra hour is a rare gift. It's prime time for friends, couples, and party crews to take on new drinking challenges and meme drinking games. Plus, with everyone a little confused about what time it actually is, you’ve got the perfect backdrop for chaos and laughs.

## The Daylight Savings Drinking Game

### What You Need

- Your favorite drinks (grab a mix for maximum fun)
- A deck of cards
- Smartphone or laptop for meme drinking games (bonus points for viral social dares)
- Timer or clock (to keep the hour wild)
- Dare challenge cards or slips (make your own or use Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder)

### How to Play

**1. Set the Vibe**  
Decorate with clocks, memes, or anything that screams “time warp.” Set the clocks back together at the exact moment daylight savings ends.

**2. Basic Rules**
- Every time someone asks, “What time is it?” everyone drinks.
- If anyone forgets to set their clock back, they do a dare challenge.
- At the top of every hour (yes, both the “real” and “fake” hour), start a new round of a classic party drinking game—think King’s Cup, Flip Cup, or Meme Roulette.

**3. Dare Challenge Rounds**
- Every 15 minutes, draw a dare card. This could be a viral meme challenge, a spicy NSFW dare (keep it legal and consensual!), or a wild house rule you make up on the spot.
- If you refuse a dare, you finish your drink.

**4. Couples and Teams Mode**
- Pair up for couples drinking games or break into teams.
- Teams compete in quickfire meme drinking games—best meme wins, loser drinks.

**5. The Final Countdown**
- As the bonus hour ends, everyone comes together for a group challenge—think viral drinking games, TikTok-worthy dares, or Drinking Dojo’s signature drinking challenges.

## House Party Ideas to Level Up the Night

- **Time Capsule Dare:** Write a dare for your future self to do next daylight savings. Seal it up!
- **Meme Wall:** Print or post your favorite meme drinking games on the wall. Complete one every time someone says “extra hour.”
- **NSFW Dare Jar:** For the brave, toss in some spicy dares for late-night chaos.
