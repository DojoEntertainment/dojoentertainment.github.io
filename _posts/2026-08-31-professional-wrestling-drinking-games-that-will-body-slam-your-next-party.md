## Game Rules: How to Play These Viral Drinking Games

This drinking game is all about reacting to the wild moments only wrestling can deliver. Here’s how it works:

### Drink When:
- A wrestler makes a dramatic entrance (bonus: chug if there are fireworks)
- Someone kicks out at two
- A commentator yells a classic catchphrase (“What a maneuver!”)
- There’s an unexpected plot twist or betrayal
- A manager interferes in the match

### Dare Challenges:
- **Meme Mayhem:** When a meme-worthy moment happens (think: someone gets thrown off a ladder), the group votes on the best meme caption. Loser takes a dare.
- **Finisher Frenzy:** When a signature move lands, everyone picks another player to challenge with a dare.
- **Promo Slam:** If someone cuts a promo (talks trash), the last person to mimic the wrestler’s voice drinks.

### Viral Drinking Game Moves:
- **Royal Rumble:** Every time a new wrestler joins a match, everyone switches seats and swaps drinks.
- **Steel Chair Shot:** If a weapon is used, everyone cheers and finishes their drink.
