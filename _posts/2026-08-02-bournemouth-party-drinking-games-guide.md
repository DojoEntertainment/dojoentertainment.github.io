## Top Bournemouth Drinking Games to Try

### 1. **Seaside Sips**

Bring the beach vibe indoors (or to your Airbnb balcony). This custom drinking game is all about celebrating Bournemouth’s coastal spirit.

**How to Play:**
- **Players:** 4+
- **What You Need:** Deck of cards, any drink, and a shell or pebble as the “Bournemouth Token”
- **Rules:**
  - Spread cards face down in a circle. Whoever draws a King gets the Bournemouth Token and can make a beach-themed rule (e.g., “Speak in your worst surfer accent for the next round”).
  - If you draw a Queen, dare someone to re-enact their wildest beach story (drink if they refuse).
  - Aces mean everyone drinks, because everyone loves the Bournemouth sunshine.
- **Why It Rocks:** Memes, accents, and dares keep the energy up.

### 2. **Pier Pressure Dare Challenge**

This one’s for the adrenaline junkies who love a good dare challenge.

**How to Play:**
- **Players:** 3+
- **What You Need:** Slips of paper, a hat or bowl, drinks, and your best mates
- **Rules:**
  - Write down wild dare challenges (think: “Find a stranger and ask for a pier selfie,” or “Do your best seagull impression”).
  - Players pull a dare from the hat. Complete it or drink.
  - Bonus: NSFW dares if you’re at a private pre-party—keep it safe and consensual!
- **Why It Rocks:** Instantly turns any night into a viral drinking game moment.

### 3. **Bournemouth Bar Crawl Bingo**

If you’re hitting the town, this game is a must.

**How to Play:**
- **Players:** Any group size
- **What You Need:** Custom bingo cards (make yours with our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)), drinks, pens
- **Rules:**
  - Fill your bingo card with classic Bournemouth sightings: “Spot a stag party,” “Someone orders a cider,” “Hear Sweet Caroline.”
  - Cross off spaces as you go—every bingo means the group does a dare or takes a shot.
- **Why It Rocks:** Great for wedding party games and house party ideas before heading out.
