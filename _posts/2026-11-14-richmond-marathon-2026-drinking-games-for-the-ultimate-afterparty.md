## Marathon Madness: Drinking Game Rules

Get your squad together and prep those cups—here’s how to play the Richmond Marathon 2026 drinking game.

### What You’ll Need

- **Beverages** (beer, seltzers, or non-alcoholic—your call)
- **Deck of cards** (optional)
- **Paper & markers** (for custom dares)
- **Shot glasses or cups**
- **Playlist of hype tracks**
- **Comfy post-race attire**

### How To Play

1. **Runner’s Roll Call:** Everyone introduces themselves with their marathon highlight. If you didn’t run, share your wildest cheer story. Anyone who forgets a detail takes a drink.

2. **Mile Marker Challenges:** For every 5 miles completed, draw a dare card. If you finished the full marathon, that’s five dares. Dares can be classic or NSFW, depending on your crowd. Need ideas? Check out [Drinking Dojo’s dare challenges](https://drinkingdojo.com).

3. **Route Relay:** Form teams. Each team picks a “runner” to complete a mini-game (like flip cup or stack the cups). Losing team drinks, winning team assigns a dare.

4. **Finish Line Toast:** Everyone makes a toast using a meme or viral quote. Anyone who repeats a toast or can’t think of one drinks.

5. **Couples Sprint:** Pair up (romantic or platonic). Complete a challenge together—sing a song, do a TikTok dance, or share your favorite house party ideas. Losing duo drinks.

6. **Wildcard Lap:** Each player writes a drinking challenge or custom dare on a slip of paper. Mix them up and draw at random. Anything goes—this is where the mayhem starts.

### Pro Tips For Marathon Drinking Games

- Stay hydrated with water breaks—marathoners know the drill!
- Use non-alcoholic options for anyone taking it easy post-race.
- Mix in meme drinking games and viral drinking games for endless laughs.
- Add wedding party games or couples drinking games if your afterparty is doubling as a celebration.
