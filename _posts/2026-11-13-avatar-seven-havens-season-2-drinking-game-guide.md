## How to Play

This drinking challenge is all about catching the best moments, wildest twists, and meme-worthy scenes as you watch Avatar Seven Havens Season 2. Every time something on the list happens, take a sip, shot, or complete a dare challenge.

### The Basic Rules

- **Choose your drink** and pour up before you start the episode.  
- **Nominate a game master** — they’ll keep everyone honest and track the rules.  
- **Pick your episode** (or binge the whole season if you’re feeling bold).  
- **Set your limits** — know when to switch to water or slow down. Play safe.  

### Sip When

- A character says “haven.”  
- Someone unleashes a new power.  
- There’s an awkward pause or dramatic stare.  
- A meme-worthy moment happens.  
- The soundtrack slaps.  

### Take a Shot When

- There’s a shocking betrayal.  
- A new haven is revealed.  
- Someone mentions the “balance.”  
- A romantic moment gets interrupted.  

### Dare Challenges

Want to crank it up? Add these dares to your party drinking games rotation. Pull from a [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) or use these:

- **NSFW dares:** The first person to laugh at a cheesy line has to DM their last meme to the group chat.  
- **Wedding party games twist:** If two characters flirt, propose a toast like you’re at a wedding.  
- **Fortnite drinking game crossover:** If there’s an epic fight scene, everyone does their best Fortnite dance — loser takes a drink.  
- **Election day drinking game style:** If there’s a vote, debate, or council meeting, everyone votes on who has to finish their drink.  

### Bonus: Meme Drinking Games

- Pause the episode at a random point. Whoever’s face is the weirdest does a dare.  
- Share the funniest meme inspired by what just happened — the winner picks someone to drink.  

## Pro Tips

- Set up a cozy viewing area — think blankets, mood lighting, and all the snacks.  
- Keep water handy for between rounds.  
- Use a custom drinking game builder to create your own rules and dares for the squad.  
- Rotate the game master each episode for fresh chaos.
