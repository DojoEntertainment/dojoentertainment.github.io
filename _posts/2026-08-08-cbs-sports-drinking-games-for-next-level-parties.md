Add in some viral drinking games, wild dare challenges, and a sprinkle of meme madness, and you’ve got a recipe for mayhem that’ll have everyone talking until the next big event.

## What You Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzer, cocktails—dealer’s choice)
- A TV or streaming device tuned to CBS Sports
- Shot glasses or cups
- Dare cards (optional, but extra spicy)
- Friends who are ready for anything

## CBS Sports Drinking Game Rules

### Basic Setup

Settle in with your squad, put the game on, and split into teams or play solo. Assign each person a drink and get ready to react to every twist, meme, and wild play.

### Classic Drinking Triggers

Take a sip every time:

- The commentators mention a player’s *“incredible work ethic.”*
- There’s a replay review or a coach’s challenge.
- A commercial features a dog or a talking animal.
- The camera cuts to a visibly stressed fan in the crowd.
- Someone shouts “Defense!” or “Let’s go!” on air.

### Take a Big Gulp or Shot When:

- There’s a game-changing play (touchdown, buzzer-beater, huge upset).
- The announcers argue about a call.
- Your team scores (double if it’s a wild underdog moment!).
- A meme-able moment hits Twitter before halftime.

### Dare Challenges

Want to crank it up? Add some dare challenges to the mix:

- Miss a trigger? Draw an NSFW dare or meme challenge from the pile.
- Most intense fan in the room must complete a dare after every turnover.
- Losing team at halftime picks a viral drinking game dare for the other team.

Get creative—try a custom drinking game builder to add your own wild rules or inside jokes!

## CBS Sports Drinking Games For Special Occasions

- **Wedding Party Games**: Set up a CBS Sports drinking game station at your reception for sports-loving guests.
- **Election Day Drinking Game**: Watching the election results on CBS? Adapt the triggers for political buzzwords and surprise outcomes.
- **Fortnite Drinking Game**: Halftime boredom? Switch the channel and do a quick round of Fortnite drinking games while you wait for the action to resume.

## Tips For a Legendary CBS Sports Drinking Game Night

- Pace yourselves—no one wants to miss the second half!
- Keep plenty of snacks and water on hand (hydration = happiness).
- Play responsibly and know your limits.
- Take plenty of photos and memes—mayhem is meant to be remembered.
