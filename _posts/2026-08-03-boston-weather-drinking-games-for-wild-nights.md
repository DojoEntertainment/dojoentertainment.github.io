## Why Boston Weather Makes the Best Drinking Game Theme

Boston’s weather is legendary for its mood swings. One minute you’re in shorts, the next you’re shoveling snow off your stoop. That unpredictability? It’s the perfect inspiration for party drinking games packed with dare challenges, meme drinking games, and viral drinking games bound to go down in group chat history.

## Boston Weather Drinking Game: The Essentials

### What You’ll Need

- At least 3 friends (the more, the merrier)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, seltzers—whatever fits the vibe)
- Phone or tablet (to check the weather app and snap meme-worthy pics)
- A deck of cards (optional, for extra twists)
- Dare cards or the [custom drinking game builder from Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) for personalized weather-themed dares

### Setting Up

1. **Choose a Weather Anchor:** Check the current Boston weather and write it on a whiteboard or group chat. This is your baseline for the night.
2. **Pick a Game Master:** Someone to keep the group honest, track dares, and throw in random weather updates.
3. **Set Your Drink Rules:** Decide on your sipping, chugging, and dare penalties upfront. (Pro tip: keep water nearby.)

## How to Play: Boston Weather Drinking Game Rules

### Basic Gameplay

1. **Weather Watch:** At the start of each hour, check the live weather. Each player guesses the next hour’s weather (rain, sun, wind, snow, “just weird”).  
   - **Correct guess:** Give out two drinks.
   - **Wrong guess:** Take one drink yourself.

2. **Forecast Fail:** If your guess is way off (like predicting sun and a blizzard hits), you must complete a dare challenge. Think NSFW dares if the crowd’s up for it, or keep it meme-worthy.  
   - Example dare: “Make a snow angel in your living room. No snow? Improvise.”

3. **Sudden Weather Alerts:** If the weather app pings with a warning (or your Game Master fakes one), everyone must chug for 10 seconds.

4. **Season Switcheroo:** If someone’s outfit doesn’t match the weather (shorts during a cold snap? Hoodie in a heatwave?), they must drink and post a meme selfie to the group chat.

5. **Wildcard Round:** Every time someone complains about the weather, the whole group drinks. Bonus points if they do it in a Boston accent.
