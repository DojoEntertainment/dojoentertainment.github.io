## How To Play The Student Loan Drinking Game

1. **Deal the Cards:**  
   Shuffle and deal cards evenly among players, or use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) for a tailored party experience.

2. **Call Out Your Debt:**  
   Each round, players confess one ridiculous thing they did to survive uni or college—think eating ramen for a week or using textbooks as pillows.

3. **Dare Challenges:**  
   Draw a card or select a dare. If you’ve done what’s on the card, drink. If not, pick another player to do a dare. Think:  
   - “Meme drinking games” (act out a viral meme or take two sips)  
   - “Student loan regret” (tell your worst online dating story or finish your drink)  
   - “Tuition challenge” (guess the cost of a random item—get it wrong, drink!)  

4. **Wild Cards:**  
   Throw in some viral drinking games and NSFW dares for bonus chaos. Examples:  
   - “Election day drinking game” rule: If someone mentions politics, everyone drinks.  
   - “Wedding party games” twist: Last person to toast to their future spouse takes two drinks.  

5. **The Graduation Round:**  
   The first player to run out of cards stands up and makes a toast to becoming debt-free. Everyone else drinks to that (because, let’s be honest, we’re all in this together).

## House Rules For Extra Fun With Student Loan Drinking Challenges

- **Default On Your Dare:**  
   Too chicken to do a dare? Drink twice.

- **Interest Accrues:**  
   Every time someone checks their phone, they add a “debt sip” to their total for the night.

- **Scholarship Challenge:**  
   Win a round of Fortnite drinking game or meme challenge to pass a drink to anyone you want.
