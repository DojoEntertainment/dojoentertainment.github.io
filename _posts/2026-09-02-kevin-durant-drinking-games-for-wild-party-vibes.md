## How To Play Kevin Durant Drinking Games

### 1. **KD’s Shot Clock Challenge**

*Inspired by the thrill of buzzer-beaters.*

**How to play:**  
- Set a 24-second timer (KD’s jersey number, anyone?)  
- Each player must take a shot or sip before the buzzer  
- Miss the timer? You’re on the hook for a dare challenge—think “post your best KD meme face” or chug for 5 seconds

### 2. **Slim Reaper Meme Game**

*Perfect for meme drinking games fans.*

**Rules:**  
- One player finds a Kevin Durant meme on their phone  
- If the group laughs, everyone drinks  
- If nobody laughs, the meme poster drinks  
- Up the stakes with NSFW dares for the biggest meme fails or viral moments

### 3. **Three-Point Dare Streak**

*For those who want some real (drinking) challenges.*

**How to play:**  
- Set up a mini basketball hoop (trash can or cup works)  
- Take turns shooting “three-pointers”  
- For every shot made, assign a dare to another player (use Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder for epic NSFW dares)  
- Miss your shot? Drink!

### 4. **KD’s Career Switcheroo**

*Test your Durantula trivia and your tolerance.*

**Rules:**  
- Name all the teams KD has played for  
- If someone misses or repeats a team, they drink  
- Add a twist: shout out a viral moment from his career—hesitate, and it’s dare time
