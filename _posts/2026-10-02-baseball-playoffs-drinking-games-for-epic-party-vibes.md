## Game 1: The Classic Baseball Playoffs Drinking Game

**What you’ll need:**
- Drinks for everyone (beer, cider, cocktails—dealer’s choice)
- A TV tuned to the playoffs
- Scorecards (optional, but fun)
- A list of dares (get creative or use our [NSFW dares](https://drinkingdojo.com) for extra spice)

**How to Play:**
1. **Assign Teams:** Split into two groups—root for opposing teams.
2. **Take a Sip When:**
    - Your team gets a hit.
    - The opposing team scores a run.
    - The announcer mentions a player’s childhood story.
    - Someone in the room shouts at a bad call.
3. **Finish Your Drink If:**
    - There’s a grand slam.
    - Your team pulls off a double play.
    - The game goes into extra innings.
4. **Dare Challenges:** After each inning, losing team pulls a dare from the pile. Keep it wild—think meme drinking games with dares like “recreate a home run celebration” or “perform your worst commentator impression.”

## Game 2: Meme Drinking Games for Baseball Playoffs

Level up your party with meme-inspired drinking games. Every time the camera cuts to a wild fan or someone spills their drink, everyone has to do a viral TikTok dance—or take a shot if they refuse. Bonus: Make your own custom drinking game builder rules on the fly for maximum mayhem.

## Couples Drinking Games: Baseball Playoffs Edition

Watching the playoffs with your partner? Try this twist:
- Every time there’s a commercial break, trade dares.
- If your favorite player strikes out, the loser does a dare selected by the other.
- Celebrate home runs with a shared drink or a couples-only challenge.

## Tips for an Unforgettable Baseball Playoffs Drinking Challenge

- Have plenty of snacks and water on hand.
- Mix in some friendly bets (loser has to do an embarrassing dare).
- Don’t forget to snap pics for social—these are the moments you’ll want to remember (or try to piece together later).
- Keep it responsible—know your limits and help friends get home safe.
