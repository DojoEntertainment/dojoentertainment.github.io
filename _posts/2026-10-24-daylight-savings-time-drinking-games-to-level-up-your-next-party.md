## Daylight Savings Time Drinking Game: The Clock’s Always Right… Right?

Here’s a custom drinking challenge to get your squad hyped as the minutes (and drinks) tick by.

### What You’ll Need

- A group of friends (4+ works best)
- Drinks of choice (beer, seltzers, cocktails, or non-alcoholic—no judgment!)
- Paper and pen or your phone for tracking points
- Optional: A big clock, timer, or a phone with an alarm

### How to Play

#### Setup

1. **Set the Mood:** Gather everyone before the time change (or right after, if you’re feeling bold).
2. **Pick a Timekeeper:** This person will be in charge of alarms, tracking points, and keeping the game on schedule.
3. **Split Into Teams:** For extra chaos, go head-to-head in teams. Solo works too!

#### The Rules

- **Every 10 Minutes:** When the timer goes off, everyone guesses what time it *feels* like (no peeking at devices). Closest guess gets to hand out a drink. Furthest off? Take a drink!
- **Spring Forward/Fall Back Blitz:** At the exact moment the clocks jump, everyone must shout “TIME WARP!” Last to shout drinks twice.
- **Dare Challenges:** Every half hour, the Timekeeper announces a dare (pull from a hat or use the [Drinking Dojo dare generator](https://drinkingdojo.com)). Complete it or double up on your next drink.
- **Meme Drinking Game:** If anyone references being tired, confused, or losing track of time (classic daylight savings memes), the whole group takes a drink. If you catch someone making a meme-worthy face, snap a pic—winner gets to skip their next drink.

#### Bonus Round

- **NSFW Dares:** Feeling brave? Add some spicy NSFW dares from Drinking Dojo’s packs, making this the daylight savings party to remember (or not).
- **Couples Challenge:** If you’re playing as couples, add a twist—if you both guess the time exactly, everyone else drinks!

## Tips to Make Your Daylight Savings Drinking Game Epic

- **House Party Ideas:** Decorate with clocks, glow sticks, or blackout curtains for true time confusion.
- **Playlist:** Queue up time-themed bangers (think “Time After Time” or “Tik Tok”).
- **Stay Safe:** Hydrate, pace yourself, and snack between rounds. Always look out for your crew.
