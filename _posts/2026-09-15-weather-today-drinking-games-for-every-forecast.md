## Weather Today Drinking Game Rules

You’ll need:
- A weather app (or just look outside)
- Your favorite drinks
- A group of friends (2+ players)
- Dare cards or slips of paper
- Optional: meme drinking games pack for extra spice

### How To Play

1. **Check the Forecast**
   - Open your weather app or peek outside. Is it sunny, cloudy, rainy, or a total mess? Each weather type unlocks certain dares and challenges.

2. **Match the Weather to the Game**
   - **Sunny**: Cheers and take a sip for every pair of sunglasses in the room. If someone mentions “perfect day,” everyone drinks.
   - **Rainy**: Every time thunder is mentioned or someone complains about the rain, take a shot. Dare someone to do their best weather reporter impression.
   - **Cloudy**: Anyone who says “cozy,” “gloomy,” or “snuggle” picks a card from the NSFW dares pile (if your crowd is up for spicy drinking challenges).
   - **Windy**: Everyone must yell “Hold onto your drinks!” last person to do it drinks. If someone’s hair gets messed up by wind, they pick a dare.
   - **Snowy/Extreme**: Break out those viral drinking games or meme drinking games for extra heat. Everyone must wear a hat or scarf—forget and you drink.

3. **Dare Challenges**
   - For every weather change during your party, draw a dare. Maybe it’s an election day drinking game challenge, or you pull a wild NSFW dare. The goal? Keep the energy up and the laughs rolling.

4. **Bonus Round: Custom Drinking Game Builder**
   - Want to make it truly next-level? Use Drinking Dojo’s [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create packs tailored to your local weather, house party ideas, or even a Fortnite drinking game if you’re stuck indoors.

## Pro Tips to Level Up

- Add regional twists: In Denmark? Make everyone say “skål” before every round. In Australia, throw in a “shrimp on the barbie” dare.
- For wedding party games, tie the weather theme into the couple’s story—like “First date in the rain? Everyone drinks to romance.”
- Turn couples drinking games into a forecast showdown: each couple bets on the next weather change; losers complete a dare.
