## How To Play MLB Trade Deadline Drinking Games

### Set Up

1. Gather your crew before or during the MLB trade deadline coverage.
2. Decide if you want to play as individuals, pairs, or teams (perfect for couples drinking games or large house parties).
3. Assign a “Commissioner” to keep track of trades, rumors, and call out drinking cues.

### The Rules

Here are some viral drinking games ideas for your MLB trade deadline bash:

#### Basic Rules

- **Take a Sip If:**
  - A trade is announced
  - A reporter says “blockbuster”
  - Your favorite team is mentioned
  - Someone mispronounces a player’s name

- **Take Two Sips If:**
  - A player finds out about a trade via social media
  - The phrase “sources say” is used

- **Finish Your Drink If:**
  - A meme-worthy trade happens (think: someone’s grandma gets traded for a bat)
  - Your team trades away a fan favorite

#### Dare Challenges

Want to up the ante? Mix in some dare challenges between trades:

- Do a viral TikTok dance if your team makes a last-minute deal
- Text your ex a baseball-themed meme (NSFW dares are welcome—if you dare)
