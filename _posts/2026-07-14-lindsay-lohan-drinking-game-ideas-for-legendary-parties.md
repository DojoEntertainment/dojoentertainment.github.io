## How To Play The Lindsay Lohan Drinking Game

### 1. Pick Your Lohan Content

Choose a classic Lindsay Lohan movie (like “Mean Girls” or “Herbie: Fully Loaded”), a playlist of her best interviews, or even her viral TikToks. The more iconic, the better!

### 2. Set The Rules For Your Party Drinking Games

Here are some party-ready rules to get you started. Take a sip or shot whenever:

- Lindsay says “fetch” or any other meme phrase.
- You spot a super early-2000s fashion choice.
- Someone references her wild child era.
- There’s a dramatic eye roll or sassy comeback.
- A scene features a party, club, or dance montage.
- You hear a Lindsay Lohan song.

Add extra spice with these dare challenges:

- **NSFW dares:** The first to laugh out loud must draw a dare card and perform it.
- **Meme drinking games:** If someone quotes a viral Lindsay meme, everyone else drinks.

### 3. Bonus Challenges To Elevate Your Drinking Challenges

Take your Lindsay Lohan drinking game to the next level:

- **Costume bonus:** Dress as your favorite Lindsay character—think Cady Heron or Anna Coleman. The best costume picks someone to take a drink.
- **Dare roulette:** Use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create Lindsay-themed dares, perfect for wedding party games or house party ideas.
- **Couples twist:** If you’re playing with your partner, try couples drinking games like “Finish the Quote” — if you fail, both of you drink.

## Pro Tips For A Legendary Night Of Viral Drinking Games

- **Pace yourself:** Lindsay’s party days were wild, but remember to drink responsibly.
- **Switch it up:** Try different movies or viral drinking games for every round.
- **Share the memories:** Snap pics, post your memes, and tag your crew — bonus points for the best viral moment.
