## How to Play the Megan Fox Drinking Game

### Step 1: Choose Your Megan Fox Content

Pick a Megan Fox movie (think Jennifer’s Body, Transformers, or Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles) or queue up her best YouTube interview moments. The game works for both movie marathons and quick binge sessions.

### Step 2: Set the Rules

Here’s where the real fun begins. Use our party drinking games template or Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder to create your own rules, or start with these classics:

#### Take One Sip When:
- Megan Fox flips her hair
- She throws a killer comeback or sarcastic line
- Someone is obviously distracted by her looks on screen
- There’s an epic action sequence

#### Take Two Sips When:
- Megan Fox’s character saves the day
- She’s in a slow-motion shot
- There’s a viral meme moment you recognize

#### Finish Your Drink or Do a Dare Challenge When:
- Megan Fox’s character kisses someone
- You spot a cameo or reference to another film she’s in
- Someone in the group can quote her line before she says it

Mix it up with meme drinking games and NSFW dares for the bold. Want to get creative? Use the custom drinking game builder at [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add your own inside jokes or friend-specific dares.

## Pro Tips for Next-Level Party Drinking Games

- **Add Dare Challenges:** Between rounds, draw a dare card. It could be anything from “reenact Megan’s most dramatic scene” to “post your best Megan Fox meme on your story.”
- **Try Couples Drinking Games:** Playing as pairs? Award bonus sips or special dares for couples who can pull off a Megan Fox movie quote in sync.
- **House Party Ideas:** Set up a “Megan Fox meme wall” where everyone posts their favourite Foxy memes throughout the night. Loser of each round has to add a new meme.
