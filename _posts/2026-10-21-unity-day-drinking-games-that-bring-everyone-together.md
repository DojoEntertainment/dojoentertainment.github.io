## The Ultimate Unity Day Drinking Game

Ready for a Unity Day drinking challenge? Gather your squad, grab your favorite drinks, and get set for a game that’s all about sharing, daring, and a little bit of chaos.

### What You’ll Need

- Drinks of choice (alcoholic or non-alcoholic, your dojo your rules)  
- A deck of cards  
- Sticky notes or paper  
- Pens/markers  
- Your phone for meme challenges  
- A sense of humor and a willingness to get wild  

### Game Setup

1. **Circle Up:** Everyone sits in a circle (classic, but it works).  
2. **Deal Cards:** Each player gets five cards.  
3. **Sticky Notes:** Everyone writes a dare or challenge for Unity Day on a sticky note and tosses it into a bowl.  
4. **Meme Ready:** Make sure at least one person in the group can access viral meme templates on their phone.  

### How to Play

- **Draw and Play:** On your turn, play a card and pull a sticky-note dare from the bowl.  
- **Dare Time:** Complete the dare—think NSFW dares, unity-themed challenges, or meme drinking games like “Recreate the Meme Face.”  
- **Skip or Sip:** If you’re not up for the dare, take a drink instead.  
- **Unity Card:** If you play a Heart, everyone has to cheers and drink together.  
- **Meme Master:** Once per round, a player can challenge the group to make the best Unity Day meme using their phone. Winner gets to assign a drink.  
- **Last Card:** First person to get rid of all their cards wins bragging rights and gets to make up an epic Unity Day dare for the group.  

### Dare Ideas for Unity Day

Not sure what to write on those sticky notes? Here’s inspiration:

- “Share your wildest party story or take two sips.”  
- “Group selfie in your best unity pose—losers drink.”  
- “NSFW dare: Text a random emoji to your ex or down your drink.”  
- “Perform a unity-themed TikTok dance.”  
- “Make up a Unity Day chant and get everyone to join in.”
