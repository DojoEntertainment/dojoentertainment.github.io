## What Is the Weather Travel Drinking Game?

Imagine combining your love for travel, unpredictable weather, and epic drinking challenges. This game is perfect for groups who want to spice things up—no matter if you’re stuck inside on a rainy night in the UK, chilling in an Aussie backyard, or pregaming a Danish festival.

## How to Play

1. **Set the Scene**  
   Pick a “Travel Guide” who will read the weather forecast (draw cards or use our challenge prompts). Shuffle your Weather Challenge Cards and place them in the middle.

2. **Draw a Card, Face the Forecast**  
   Players take turns drawing a card and reading their weather scenario out loud. Each card triggers a unique dare, drinking challenge, or meme-worthy moment.

3. **Complete the Challenge or Drink**  
   If you bail on the challenge, take a sip. If you ace it, nominate someone else to drink. Some cards have “Storm Warnings”—group dares where everyone drinks.

4. **Keep the Weather Rolling**  
   First one to survive five weather scenarios without backing out is the Weather Travel Champion. Bonus points if you make it through without spilling your drink.

## Example Weather Challenge Cards

- **Sunny in Sydney**: Share your wildest travel story or finish your drink.
- **Rainstorm in London**: Everyone speaks in a British accent until your next turn. Break character? Drink.
- **Canadian Blizzard**: Last person to name a Canadian city drinks.
- **Danish Downpour**: Do your best rain dance. If the group isn’t impressed, bottoms up!
- **Aussie Heatwave**: Dare someone to do a “shoey” (drink from their shoe)—or take a penalty shot.
- **Thunderstorm**: Shout out a viral meme. If no one laughs, you drink.
- **Foggy Airport Delay**: Swap seats with another player. If you land in the “exit row” (last seat), take two sips.

## Optional NSFW and Couples Rules

- **NSFW Dares**: For parties wanting a bit more spice, add some wild dares like “Text your ex a weather emoji” or “Show your last vacation photo.”
- **Couples Drinking Games**: If you’re playing in pairs, mix in dares that require teamwork—like “Couple with the best travel selfie makes the other pairs drink.”
