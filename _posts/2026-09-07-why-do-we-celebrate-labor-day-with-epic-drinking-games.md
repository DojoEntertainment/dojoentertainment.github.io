## What’s the Real Deal With Labor Day?

Labor Day started as a way to recognize the working legends who built our cities, powered our industries, and basically made modern life possible. In the US and Canada, it’s a salute to the labor movement and all the good things that came with it—think weekends, fair wages, and safe workplaces. The UK, Australia, and Denmark all have their own versions of this celebration, but the vibe is the same: respect for the grind, and a chance to kick back with friends.

And honestly, after all that work, who *doesn’t* deserve a little mayhem?

## Level Up Your Labor Day With Drinking Games

Labor Day isn’t just about saying thanks—it’s about making the most of your freedom. That means ditching boring parties and bringing the heat with party drinking games, meme drinking games, and dare challenges that keep everyone laughing (and maybe blushing).

Here are three unforgettable drinking games perfect for your Labor Day bash:

### 1. Meme Kings

**What You Need:**  
- A deck of meme cards (or DIY with your favorite memes printed out)  
- Your favorite drinks

**How to Play:**  
- Each round, one player is the “Meme King” and picks a scenario (e.g., “When you realize tomorrow is Monday”).  
- Everyone else throws down the meme card they think fits best.  
- The Meme King picks the winner, who gives out two drinks. The least funny card? That player takes a drink.  
- Rotate the Meme King each round and keep the laughs rolling.

### 2. Dare Roulette

**What You Need:**  
- A spinning app or a bottle for classic spin-the-bottle  
- Pre-written NSFW dares (or keep it clean if that’s your vibe)  
- Drinks for everyone

**How to Play:**  
- Spin to choose who gets a dare.  
- The chosen one must complete the dare or take two drinks.  
- Want to spice it up? Let the group vote on custom dare challenges using our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com).

### 3. Labor Day Legends

**What You Need:**  
- Sticky notes or index cards  
- Pens  
- Drinks, obviously

**How to Play:**  
- Each player writes down a historical labor movement or job-related moment on a card.  
- Shuffle and hand them out.  
- Players give charades-style hints about their moment.  
- Guess right, choose someone to drink. Guess wrong, you drink!

This game is perfect for mixing a bit of trivia with your party drinking games, and it’s a guaranteed crowd-pleaser at any house party.
