## US Open Results Drinking Game Rules

### Basic Game Setup

1. **Pick Your Players**  
   Everyone chooses a tennis player or doubles team to root for during the match. If your player is knocked out, you’re on dare duty.

2. **Prepare Your Drinks**  
   Stock up with your favorite party fuel. Bonus points for themed cocktails (Serena Spritz, anyone?).

3. **Set Up Dare Challenges**  
   Have a stack of NSFW dares ready, plus a few meme-worthy drinking challenges for the brave (or reckless).

### How to Play

#### **Serve-and-Sip**

- Take a sip every time your chosen player wins a point.
- Double sip for an ace or wild rally.

#### **Break Point Blitz**

- If your player hits a break point, everyone else drinks.
- If your player gets broken, you finish your drink.

#### **Upset Alert**

- If an underdog beats a favorite according to the US Open results, everyone in the favorite’s camp must complete a dare challenge.

#### **Tiebreaker Terrors**

- When a match goes into a tiebreak, everyone draws a dare card. Complete it or take a shot.

#### **Wildcard Wagers**

- Before the match, make bets on final scores, sets, or wild predictions (like a player smashing a racket). Losers face a meme drinking game round—think “never have I ever” but with tennis fails.
