## Fairground Frenzy: The Ultimate Maryland State Fair Drinking Game

### What You Need

- A group of friends (the more, the merrier)
- Your favorite drinks (bonus points for local Maryland brews)
- Phone or notepad for tracking points and dares
- Drinking Dojo [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) (for next-level fun)

### How to Play

1. **Pick Your Game Zones:**  
   Divide the fair into *zones*—think food stalls, animal exhibits, carnival rides, and concert areas.

2. **Draw Dare Cards:**  
   Use the Drinking Dojo app to generate dare challenges or NSFW dares for each zone. Or, create your own using our custom builder. 

3. **Complete the Challenge:**  
   At each zone, one person draws a dare. If they complete it, everyone else drinks. If they chicken out, *they* drink. Simple.

4. **Meme It Up:**  
   Snap a meme-worthy pic or video of the dare. Share with your group (or the ‘gram) for bonus points.

5. **Final Showdown:**  
   At the end of the night, tally up points for completed dares and viral drinking games moments. The winner gets bragging rights—loser does a walk of shame in front of the prize-winning pig.

### Example Dare Challenges

- Try to win a giant stuffed animal—if you fail, chug your drink.
- Ask a stranger for their favorite fair food—if it’s fried, everyone drinks.
- Start a mini dance-off at the concert area. Refuse? Take two drinks.
- Create a meme with the wildest livestock you can find.
- Find someone in a ridiculous hat—trade hats or finish your drink.

## Extra Ideas for Couples and Groups

- **Couples Drinking Games:**  
  Challenge your date to a food taste test (loser drinks), or team up for a ride-based dare (like screaming the loudest on the Ferris wheel).
- **Wedding Party Games:**  
  If you’re rolling deep for a pre-wedding bash, split into teams and see who can rack up the most dares.
- **House Party Ideas:**  
  Can’t make it to the fair? Recreate the vibes at home with themed decorations and custom drinking game packs from Drinking Dojo. Bonus: Try adding a *fortnite drinking game* twist for the afterparty.

## Pro Tips for a Legendary Maryland State Fair Drinking Game

- Pace yourself—hydration is key, especially if it’s hot out.
- Respect the fair rules and keep it fun (no getting kicked out, please!).
- Always have a designated driver or plan for a safe ride home.
