Election day isn’t just about ballots and breaking news—it's also one of the wildest nights to gather your crew, throw on some campaign memes, and turn democracy into the ultimate party drinking game. Whether you’re team red, blue, or just here for the dare challenges, these election day drinking games will keep your spirits high (literally) from the first poll update to the final results.

## Why Election Day Is Perfect for Drinking Games

Let’s face it—watching the results roll in can get tense. So why not channel all that nervous energy into viral drinking games, meme drinking games, and dare challenges that’ll leave you with unforgettable memories (and maybe a few blurry ones)? With the right rules and a bit of friendly competition, election night can become the highlight of your year.

- Your drink of choice (mocktails count too)

- Voting night bingo cards (easy to make or download)

## The Ultimate Election Day Drinking Game: Rules and How to Play

### 1. **Election Coverage Bingo**

Print or create bingo cards with common election day phrases, candidate quirks, or news graphics. (Example: “Swing state,” “historic turnout,” “awkward Zoom interview,” “polls close in…”)

**How to Play:**
- Everyone gets a bingo card.
- When you spot or hear an item, take a sip.
- First to get bingo picks someone for a dare challenge or meme drinking game round.

### 2. **NSFW Dares for Brave Voters**

Ready to spice things up? Add some NSFW dares to the mix—if you’re game. Every time your candidate wins a state, you assign a dare to someone else. Losers have to complete the dare or take a shot.

### 3. **Election Results Drinking Challenge**

- Take a sip every time a news anchor says, “too close to call.”
- Finish your drink if your least favorite candidate wins a key state.
- Try a fun dare challenge every time there’s a technical glitch or awkward silence.
