## How To Play: Walmart Black Friday 2026 Drinking Games

### The Basics

This party drinking game is all about celebrating the madness of Black Friday—Walmart style. The rules are simple, but the fun ramps up fast. 

### Game Setup

1. Gather your group and assign everyone a number.
2. Decide who’s the “Deal Master”—they’ll announce deals, memes, or news as they pop up.
3. Each player grabs their drink and gets ready for the challenge.

### The Rules

- **Take One Sip When:**
  - Someone mentions “Rollback” or “Doorbuster”
  - You see an inflatable product on sale
  - A meme about Black Friday chaos drops in your group chat

- **Take Two Sips When:**
  - Someone in your group gets a deal on a TV or game console
  - You spot a Fortnite drinking game reference in a social post
  - You witness someone running through a Walmart aisle (video counts!)

- **Take Three Sips When:**
  - The Deal Master finds a product with over 50% off
  - A celebrity is spotted in a Black Friday meme
  - Anyone shares an NSFW dare or meme (bonus: complete the dare for immunity)

- **Finish Your Drink If:**
  - The site crashes, your cart empties, or you lose out on a deal you wanted
  - Someone in your group rage-quits the shopping session

### Dare Challenges

Spice things up with classic dare challenges. Every time a player lands on a “Double Dare” (randomly called out by the Deal Master), they must:
- Post a Black Friday meme with their best “bargain hunter” face
- Try to convince someone in another group chat to join the game
- Create a custom drinking game rule for the next round
