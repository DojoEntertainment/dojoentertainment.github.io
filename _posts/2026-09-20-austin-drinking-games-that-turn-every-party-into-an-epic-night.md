## Classic Austin Drinking Game: Sixth Street Shuffle

**What you need:**  
- A deck of cards  
- Drinks (your choice — local craft beer, cocktails, or whatever’s on hand)

**How to play:**  
- Everyone sits in a circle and takes turns drawing a card. Each card equals a different dare challenge or rule, inspired by the chaos of Austin’s iconic Sixth Street.  
- Make up your own rules or try these crowd favorites:

  - **Ace:** Start a chant — everyone else drinks until they join in.
  - **2:** Pick someone to take two sips.
  - **3:** Do your best cowboy impression or drink.
  - **4:** Create a quick meme using your phone. Loser (judged by the group) drinks.
  - **5:** Social — everyone drinks!
  - **6:** Shout “Keep Austin Weird” as loud as you can or finish your drink.

Play until the deck runs out — or everyone’s ready to hit up a taco truck.

## Dare Challenges: Austin Edition

Ready to crank up the party? Mix in some wild dare challenges that scream Austin energy. Here are some ideas:

- **ATX Accent Challenge:** Try your best Texan drawl while giving a toast. Fail? Drink.
- **Festival Flash:** Create a festival headband out of whatever’s around. If it falls off before your next turn, chug.
- **Meme Drinking Game:** Everyone sends an Austin-themed meme to the group chat. Best gets to make someone finish their drink. Worst? NSFW dares are coming your way.
