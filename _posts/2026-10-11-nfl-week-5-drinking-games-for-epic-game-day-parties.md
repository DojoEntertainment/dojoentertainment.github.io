### Basic Rules

Pick your teams and grab your drinks. Every time one of the following events happens during NFL week 5, take the assigned action. Easy to play, impossible to forget.

#### First Quarter

- **Touchdown:** Everyone on the *scoring* team takes 2 sips. Opposing fans take 1 sip out of “respect.”  
- **Turnover:** All losing team fans finish their drink if their team turns the ball over.  
- **Field Goal Miss:** Last person to boo takes a dare challenge.  

#### Second Quarter

- **Commercial Break:** Quick dare challenge or meme drinking game. Use our custom builder for wild prompts.  
- **Penalty Flag:** All players guess the penalty. Anyone wrong drinks.  
- **Coach Challenge:** Everyone picks red or blue. If the flag matches your choice, give out 2 sips.  

#### Halftime

- **Halftime Showdown:** Play a mini meme drinking game—think TikTok dances or best touchdown celebration. Loser does an NSFW dare (if you’re brave).  

#### Third Quarter

- **Interception:** Opposing fans take 3 sips and share a meme in the group chat.  
- **Big Play (20+ yards):** Everyone drinks if their fantasy player is involved.  
- **Ref Makes a Bad Call:** Group vote—if majority says “bad,” everyone drinks.  

#### Fourth Quarter

- **Go-Ahead Score:** Losing team fans chug for 5 seconds.  
- **Game Tied:** All players toast and sip together—good vibes only.  
- **Last-Minute Field Goal:** Everyone stands and drinks until the kick is made or missed.
