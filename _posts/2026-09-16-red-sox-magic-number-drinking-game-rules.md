## What Is the Red Sox Magic Number Drinking Game?

It’s a party drinking game inspired by the thrill of counting down the Boston Red Sox's magic number—the number of wins (or rival losses) needed to clinch a playoff spot. But instead of just tracking stats, you’ll be tracking sips, dares, and meme-worthy antics with every play. Perfect for house parties, wedding party games, or just vibing with friends on a random Tuesday.

## How To Play

1. **Set the Magic Number**  
   Before the game starts, announce the current Red Sox magic number (you can Google it if you’re not sure). This number is your target for the night.

2. **Assign Numbers**  
   Each player claims a number between 1 and the magic number. If you have fewer players than the magic number, players can have multiple numbers.

3. **Drink When Your Number Comes Up**  
   Every time the Red Sox score a run or make a notable play, subtract one from the magic number. When your number is called, you drink!  
   *Example: If the magic number is 5 and the Sox score, the number drops to 4. Whoever has 4 drinks!*

4. **Dare Challenges for Extra Fun**  
   Every time the magic number hits an even number, the player with that number completes a dare challenge. Pull one from your NSFW dares deck or make up a meme drinking game dare on the spot.

5. **Viral Drinking Games Bonus**  
   If the Red Sox hit a home run, everyone must post a meme-worthy selfie or video to their group chat (or TikTok, if you’re feeling bold) while chugging their drink.

6. **The Last Sip**  
   When the magic number reaches 1, everyone does a group cheers and finishes their drink. If the Sox clinch, the party goes wild—queue up more drinking challenges or switch to another fan-favorite like the [Fortnite drinking game](https://drinkingdojo.com).

## House Rules and Customizations

- **Couples Drinking Games Twist:** Pair up and share numbers. You’ll be rooting for (or against) each other all night.
- **Wedding Party Games Edition:** Use the bride and groom’s favorite team instead of the Sox, or create custom dares based on wedding inside jokes.
- **Election Day Drinking Game Mode:** Swap out baseball stats for election results—every update, take a sip and complete a dare!
