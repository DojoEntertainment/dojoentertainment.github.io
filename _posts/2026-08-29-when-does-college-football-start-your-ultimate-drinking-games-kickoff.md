### So, When Does College Football Start?

Mark your calendars: the NCAA college football season usually kicks off in late August, with Week 0 games getting the party started, and Week 1 bringing in the big matchups the following week. Whether you’re in the States, the UK, Canada, Australia, or Denmark, you can catch the action and let the games—and the drinks—begin.

### Why College Football and Drinking Games Are the Perfect Match

There’s just something about college football that pairs perfectly with ice-cold drinks and rowdy dare challenges. From meme drinking games to custom drinking game builders, the possibilities for wild fun are endless.

### The Ultimate College Football Drinking Game

Let’s get real: the classic beer pong and flip cup are fun, but why not spice things up with a college football-themed drinking game? Here’s a go-to guide for turning any college football watch party into a legendary night.

#### What You’ll Need

- Drinks of choice (beer, seltzer, cocktails—whatever your squad loves)  
- TV or streaming device tuned to the game  
- Game sheet or access to the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)  
- Friends who are ready to play (and maybe a couple of snacks for survival)  

#### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Teams:** Divide your crew into two groups, each rooting for the opposing teams.  
2. **Game On:** Every time one of these classic football moments happens, take a drink or complete a dare:  

   - **Touchdown:** Everyone cheers and drinks!  
   - **Field Goal:** Last person to yell “Goal!” does a dare.  
   - **Turnover:** The group supporting the losing team takes two drinks.  
   - **Commercial Break:** Play a quick round of meme drinking games or NSFW dares.  
   - **Penalty Flag:** Everyone guesses the penalty. Wrong? Bottoms up.  
   - **Coach Challenge:** Make up a drinking challenge on the spot using the custom builder.  
3. **Halftime Mayhem:** At halftime, the team with the least points does a group dare challenge.  

#### Dare Challenges and Meme Drinking Games

Mix things up by tossing in viral drinking games or dare challenges between quarters. Get creative with:

- **NSFW dares** (for after-dark parties)  
- **Wedding party games** (if your crew is into double-duty celebrations)  
- **Couples drinking games** (for flirty rivalry)  
- **House party ideas** like “Fortnite drinking game” for the off-season  

### Safety First, Always

We’re all about unforgettable nights and bending the rules, but your safety—and the safety of your friends—comes first. Know your limits, keep water handy, and never drink and drive.
