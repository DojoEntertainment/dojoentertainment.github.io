## Why Make Your Delay a Party?

Sure, you could scroll TikTok or nap on those uncomfortable benches, but why not transform a travel headache into a legendary story? ### What You Need

- A drink (airport bar, water bottle, or that mystery juice from your carry-on)  
- A phone or notepad (to keep score or track dares)  
- Your friends, new or old  
- A sense of humor and willingness to get a little silly  

> **Pro tip:** Always play responsibly, respect local laws, and know your limits!

## 1. Flight Delay Dare Challenge

**Perfect for:** Groups of 2–8, waiting at the gate or airport lounge  
**How to play:**  
- Each person writes down a dare related to airport life (e.g., "Ask a stranger for their favorite travel tip," "Speak in an accent for 10 minutes," "Get a selfie with a pilot").  
- Fold the dares and toss them in a hat (or use the Drinking Dojo [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to randomize).  
- Take turns drawing a dare. Complete it or take a drink.  
- Bonus: If someone gets the courage to do a wild dare, everyone else drinks!

## 2. Boarding Group Bingo

**Perfect for:** Solo travelers or competitive friends  
**How to play:**  
- Make a quick bingo card with things like "Someone runs to the gate," "Gate change announced," "Free snacks appear," "Baby starts crying."  
- Mark off squares as events happen. First to bingo assigns a dare or drinks.  
- Spice it up with meme drinking games rules—think "Take a drink every time you hear the words 'weather delay.'"

## 3. Suitcase Roulette

**Perfect for:** Any group size, great for house party ideas when you’re back home, too  
**How to play:**  
- Each person picks an item from their carry-on or suitcase.  
- Place all items in the middle. Blindfold a player and have them pick one.  
- Whoever owns the item shares an embarrassing travel story or takes a drink.  
- Add NSFW dares for extra spice (just maybe not in front of airport staff).

## 4. Wedding Party Games on the Fly

**Perfect for:** Destination wedding groups stranded together  
**How to play:**  
- Assign everyone a wedding-related role (bride, best man, guest, etc.).  
- Every time your role is called over the loudspeaker (or you hear your name in conversation), take a drink or complete a dare.  
- Great way to get the wedding party vibing before you even land.

## 5. Couples Drinking Games for Delayed Duos

**Perfect for:** Couples or travel buddies  
**How to play:**  
- Take turns asking each other “Would You Rather” questions about travel mishaps.  
- If your partner guesses your answer wrong, they drink—if right, you drink.  
- Mix in a few dare challenges for extra laughs.
