## How to Play the Emmy Rossum Drinking Game

This party drinking game is all about spotting classic Emmy Rossum moments and capitalizing on the wild, meme-worthy chaos. Here’s how to set it up:

### 1. Choose Your Emmy Rossum Content

Pick a show or movie featuring Emmy Rossum. *Shameless* is the obvious go-to, but you can mix it up with her movie roles for bonus points.

### 2. Set the Rules

Every time one of the following happens on screen, everyone drinks! Get ready for some serious dare challenges and meme drinking games energy:

- **Fiona (Emmy) has to clean up someone else’s mess:** Take 1 sip  
- **Someone says "Gallagher":** Take 1 sip  
- **Fiona stands up for her family:** Take 2 sips  
- **Epic meltdown or kitchen table rant:** Take 3 sips  
- **Unexpected song performance:** Everyone finishes their drink  
- **Iconic Emmy eye roll:** Do a dare challenge from the pile (or make up your own on the spot)  

### 3. Add Dare Challenges

Level up your party drinking games by tossing in NSFW dares or meme dares every time someone skips their drink or refuses to complete a challenge. Rotate who comes up with the dare to keep things unpredictable.

### 4. Go Viral with Custom Rules

Want to make it truly your own? Use a [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add inside jokes, regional slang (hello UK and Aussie legends), or even add wedding party games twists for special occasions.

## Pro Tips for Maximum Mayhem

- Switch up the game with couples drinking games rules—pair up and double the dares.  
- For competitive friends, track who’s skipped the most drinks and crown them the “Fiona of the Night” (with a meme-worthy dare as their prize).  
- If you’re feeling bold, try a Fortnite drinking game or inauguration day drinking game after your Emmy round for back-to-back fun.
