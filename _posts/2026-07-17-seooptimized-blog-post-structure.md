### How to Play This Party Drinking Game

**1. Set the Mood**  
Queue up a Morgan Wallen set or livestream his Baltimore performance. Make sure everyone has a full drink and is ready to sing along!

**2. The Rules**  
Every time one of these things happens, you drink. Simple, wild, and guaranteed to bring the mayhem:

- **Take a sip if:**
  - Wallen mentions “whiskey,” “Tennessee,” or “Bartender”
  - Someone in the group sings (or shouts) the wrong lyrics
  - You spot a cowboy hat in the crowd or on TV

- **Take two sips if:**
  - Wallen talks about heartbreak, exes, or small towns
  - Someone requests “Last Night” or “Sand in My Boots”
  - A meme-worthy dance breaks out (TikTok moves count!)

- **Finish your drink or take a shot if:**
  - Wallen brings out a special guest—bonus if it’s a Baltimore native
  - The crowd goes wild and starts a chant
  - Anyone in your group tries (and fails) to hit a high note

- **Dare Challenges:**
  - Draw a dare card (create your own, or use Drinking Dojo’s custom dare pack)
  - Example NSFW dares: Share your wildest concert story, send a snap singing Wallen lyrics, or try a TikTok dance

**3. Make It Viral**  
Snap a group selfie every time you finish a round. Tag it #MorganWallenBaltimore and challenge friends to beat your score. Who knows? Your meme drinking game moment could go viral.
