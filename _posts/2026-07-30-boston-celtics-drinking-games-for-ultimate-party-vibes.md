## Game One: Celtics Shot Clock

This meme drinking game is perfect for keeping everyone on their toes. It’s simple, fast-paced, and works for any Celtics game—regular season or playoffs.

### How to Play

1. **Set a timer** for every 5 minutes of game play.
2. When the timer goes off, everyone takes a sip.
3. Add in wild dare challenges from the Drinking Dojo pack—whoever’s team is losing at the buzzer must complete a dare.

### Bonus Dare Ideas

- Take a shot every time a Celtics player makes a 3-pointer.
- Do an NSFW dare if the Celtics have a 10-point lead.
- Swap seats with someone every time the Celtics call a timeout.

### Sample Dares

- Make a meme about the game in real time—loser drinks.
- Try to spell “Antetokounmpo” (if the Celtics are playing the Bucks) or accept a drinking challenge.

## Game Three: Halftime Hoops

Halftime is your chance to get creative. Set up a mini hoop (or trash can and paper ball) and host a shooting contest. Each miss = a drink. Winner gets to assign a dare to any player at the party.
