## What Is Bumble?

Bumble is a quick-fire, action-packed party drinking game that gets everyone talking—and drinking. Players sit in a circle and compete to avoid being the “bumble bee” by paying attention, reacting fast, and keeping up with escalating dare challenges. It’s silly, it’s social, and it’s customizable with Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder for even wilder rounds.

## What You Need

- A group of fun-loving adults (3+ players recommended)
- A deck of cards or Drinking Dojo’s custom dare cards (optional for twists)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, seltzers—whatever your crew prefers)
- A “bee” object (like a yellow coaster, hat, or anything that says “bumble”)

## How To Play Bumble

### The Setup

1. **Sit in a circle.** Place the “bee” in the center.
2. **Nominate a Bumble Master.** This person starts the game and keeps things buzzing.

### Basic Rules

1. **Start the Buzz:** The Bumble Master taps the “bee” and says “Bumble to [Player’s Name]!”
2. **Pass It On:** That player has 2 seconds to tap the bee and say “Bumble to [another player’s name]!” If they hesitate or mess up, they drink.
3. **Dare Challenges:** Anyone can shout “Honeycomb!” at any point, triggering a dare challenge:
   - The Bumble Master draws a dare card or uses the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create a unique NSFW dare or meme challenge.
   - The chosen player must complete the dare or take two drinks.
4. **Add Meme Mayhem:** Between rounds, throw in viral drinking game twists (like imitating a meme, silly accents, or Fortnite dance-offs for a forfeit).

### Extra Fun Variations

- **Couples Drinking Games Twist:** Pair up and play as teams—if one partner fails, both drink.
- **Wedding Party Games Mode:** Add wedding-themed dares (e.g., “Give a toast to the couple”).
- **House Party Ideas Bonus:** The slowest player each round becomes the next Bumble Master.
