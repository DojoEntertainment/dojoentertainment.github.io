## Olivia Rodrigo Tour Drinking Game: The Rules

Ready to upgrade your concert experience? Here’s how to play:

### 1. **The Song Sip-Off**
- Every time Olivia drops a lyric you screamed in your car, take a sip.
- If she performs a surprise song, everyone finishes their drink.

### 2. **Dare Challenges**
- When someone in your group messes up the lyrics, they pull a dare card. (Bonus points if you use NSFW dares for the 21+ crowd!)
- Dare ideas: Serenade your group with your best Olivia impression, post an embarrassing meme in your group chat, or do your best concert dance move.

### 3. **Meme Drinking Game Moment**
- Every time a viral meme from the tour pops up (think “drivers license” tears or that iconic purple dress), everyone drinks.
- Anyone caught texting their ex during “traitor” must do a double dare.

### 4. **Couples or Friends Challenge**
- If you’re playing as couples or besties, compete in a lyric-off. Winner gives out drinks; loser takes a dare.

### 5. **Concert Bingo**
- Make a bingo card with classic Olivia tour moments: “anguish face during ballad,” “fan with a wild sign,” “confetti drop,” etc.
- First to bingo picks someone to finish their drink.
