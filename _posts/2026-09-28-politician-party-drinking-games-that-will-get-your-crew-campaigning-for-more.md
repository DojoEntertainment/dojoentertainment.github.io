## The Politician Drinking Game Blueprint

Here’s how you can turn any political event — or just a chill night with friends — into a legendary bash.

### What You’ll Need

- A group of friends (the more, the merrier)
- Drinks of choice (beer, cocktails, mocktails — your call)
- Access to a political debate, election night coverage, or even YouTube clips of politicians
- Dare cards or your phone for our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)
- Optional: meme props, campaign buttons, or silly hats for bonus laughs

### How to Play

**Pick Your Politicians:**  
Before you start, each person picks a politician. This will be their “candidate” for the night.

**Set Up Your House Rules:**  
Decide on your basic rules and what counts as a “trigger” — classic choices include:
- Every time your politician says their favorite catchphrase, everyone else drinks.
- If a meme-worthy moment happens (think wild hand gestures or awkward pauses), everyone drinks.
- When someone tries to dodge a question, assign a dare challenge from the pile.
- NSFW dares for when things get spicy — perfect for late-night party drinking games.

**Election Day or Inauguration Day Variations:**  
If you’re playing during a real event (like an election day drinking game or an inauguration day drinking game), add these:
- Chug if your candidate gets roasted by the moderator.
- Take a shot if there’s a technical glitch or someone forgets their mic is hot.
- Down your drink if someone storms off or throws shade.

**Wedding Party Games Twist:**  
Hosting a wedding rager? Assign politicians to tables and let everyone compete for the title of “Most Likely to Run for Office.” Winner gets a dare or custom meme drinking game challenge!

### Dare Challenges to Spice It Up

Need inspiration? Try these:
- Impersonate your politician for 60 seconds — best impression wins immunity for the next round.
- Create a campaign slogan on the spot; loser drinks.
- NSFW dare: Read a ridiculous policy proposal in your best politician voice.
- Make a meme using a party photo and send it to the group chat; loser drinks.
