## The Best Daylight Savings Drinking Game

Ready to make the most of that extra (or missing) hour? Here’s a party drinking game from the Drinking Dojo playbook—perfect for any squad, from dorm rooms to destination weddings.

### What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (mix it up: beer, cocktails, or non-alcoholic if you prefer)
- A group of friends (the more, the merrier)
- A clock or phone with a timer
- A pack of *dare challenges* (or use the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create your own)
- Optional: meme cards or viral drinking game prompts

### How To Play: “Time Warp”

1. **Set The Mood**  
   Gather everyone in the same room and set your clocks/phones to the same time. Announce that for the next hour, the only time that matters is “party time.”

2. **Draw Your Hour**  
   Each player draws a card/challenge from the dare pile. If you’re using the custom drinking game builder, load up your personalized dares, NSFW dares, or meme drinking games.

3. **Time Check**  
   Every 15 minutes, pause the music and do a group dare. The person with the least wild performance drinks. (Pro tip: use viral trends or memes for extra laughs.)

4. **Clock Confusion**  
   At random intervals, shout “TIME WARP!” Everyone must switch seats. The last person to sit drinks and pulls a new dare challenge.

5. **Couples Twist**  
   Playing with couples? Pair up for some couples drinking games—like answering funny questions about each other. Wrong answer? Both drink!

6. **Final Countdown**  
   When the hour is up (or just before the clocks change), everyone chooses their favorite dare and nominates someone to do it again. The group votes on the MVP of the night.

### House Rules To Keep It Wild
- Extra drink for anyone who forgets if the clock goes forward or back.
- If someone mentions “daylight savings” or complains about sleep—double dare!
