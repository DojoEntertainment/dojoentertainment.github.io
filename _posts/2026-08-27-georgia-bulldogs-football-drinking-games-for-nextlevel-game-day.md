- Last person to shout “GO DAWGS!” drinks.

#### Opponent Scores

#### Missed Field Goal
- Whoever called the miss takes a dare challenge.

#### Halftime

## Top Dare Challenges to Keep the Energy High at Your Bulldogs Watch Party

- **“Fortnite Bulldogs”**: Last person to floss when the Bulldogs score does a shot.
- **“Meme Face-Off”**: During a coach close-up, everyone must make their best meme face. Loser drinks.
- **“Election Day Vibes”**: Everyone votes on the next dare. Most votes picks who drinks.
- **“Wedding Party Toast”**: Make up a ridiculous toast for the Bulldogs. Best toast gives out a drink.
