## Classic Premier League Drinking Game

### What You Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, cocktails, or non-alcoholic if you prefer)
- A TV or stream set to the Premier League match
- Printed schedule or a phone to check fixtures
- Dare challenges (optional: spice it up with [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com))

### How to Play

**1. Pre-Game Setup:**  
Gather everyone and pick teams to support. If the group is split, assign teams at random. Decide on your drink of choice and make sure everyone has their glass ready.

**2. The Rules:**  
Drink every time one of the following happens:
- **Goal**: Everyone supporting the team that conceded takes a drink.
- **Yellow Card**: The person who last touched something yellow takes a drink.
- **Red Card**: Everyone finishes their current drink.
- **VAR Check**: Dare challenge time—pick a meme drinking game dare for someone in the group.
- **Missed Penalty**: Everyone supporting the kicker’s team does a group dare.
- **Injury Timeout**: Last person to stand up drinks.

**3. Bonus Round:**  
At halftime, play a quick round of NSFW dares if you’re feeling brave (keep it friendly and fun!).  
If your team scores a stoppage-time goal, you get to make a new rule for the next 10 minutes.

Think TikTok dances, bad football impressions, or singing your anthem at full volume.
