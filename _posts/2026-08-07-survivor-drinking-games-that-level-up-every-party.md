Ready to outdrink, outwit, and outlast your friends? Survivor drinking games take the classic elimination vibe and crank it up with wild dares, custom rules, and meme-worthy moments. Whether you’re chilling in a dorm, throwing a house party, or spicing up a weekend with your crew, this epic party drinking game is your ticket to unforgettable nights.

## What Is Survivor Drinking Game?

Survivor drinking games transform your party into an all-out competition. Think of it as a battle of wits, luck, and (of course) drinking skills. Players face off in challenges, complete dare challenges, and vote each other out until only one survivor remains. There are tons of ways to play, and you can use meme drinking games, custom drinking game builders, or even themed viral drinking games to make it your own.

## What You’ll Need

- A group of 5 or more players (the more, the messier)
- A deck of cards or slips of paper for votes
- Drinks of choice (beer, cocktails, seltzers—your call)
- A list of dares or challenge cards (bonus points for NSFW dares)
- Pen and paper for keeping track of votes and winners

## How To Play Survivor Drinking Game

### 1. Set Up the Tribe

- Gather your friends around and make sure everyone has a drink in hand.
- Decide if you want to play as one group or split into teams (tribes) for bigger parties.

### 2. Kick Off the Challenges

- The game is played in rounds. Each round features a drinking challenge or dare.
- Use a custom drinking game builder or pre-made viral drinking games packs for challenge ideas, like flip cup, truth-or-drink, or meme-inspired dares.
- Everyone attempts the challenge. Whoever loses the round must drink and is up for elimination.

### 3. The Voting Ceremony

- After each challenge, everyone secretly votes for a player to be eliminated.
- Use cards, slips of paper, or even a group chat for voting.
- The player with the most votes is “banished” but sticks around as the judge for the next round (they decide who wins ties or challenge disputes).

### 4. Keep It Going

- Continue with new challenges and voting rounds until only two players remain.
- For the final round, bring back the eliminated players as a “jury” to vote for the ultimate survivor based on who played the hardest, had the wildest dares, or just kept the party going.

### 5. Crown the Ultimate Survivor

- The survivor earns bragging rights—plus, you can create a tradition where the winner picks the next party drinking game or dares for the group.

## Survivor Drinking Game Ideas

- **Wedding party games:** Spice up a bachelor or bachelorette with themed dares.
- **Couples drinking games:** Pair up and compete in teams for a hilarious date night.
- **House party ideas:** Make each challenge a different room or theme.
- **NSFW dares:** For the brave, toss in some wild dares to up the ante.
- **Themed rounds:** Try a Fortnite drinking game round or an inauguration day drinking game for a timely twist.

## Pro Tips for Epic Survivor Nights

- Always play responsibly and know your limits.
- Swap out drinks for water or soft drinks if you want a “hydration round.”
- Use meme drinking games or viral drinking games packs to keep the energy high and laughter rolling.

### Can I include NSFW dares in Survivor drinking games?

Absolutely! NSFW dares add a wild twist to your party drinking games. Just make sure all players are comfortable with the content to keep the vibe fun and inclusive.
