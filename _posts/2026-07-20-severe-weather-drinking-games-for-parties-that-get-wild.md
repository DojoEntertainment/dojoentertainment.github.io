## How to Play Severe Weather Drinking Games

This game is all about channeling the chaos of the storm. Every time the weather outside rages—or you hear thunder in your playlist—the party gets a new dare or challenge. Here’s how to get started:

### Set Up Your Drinking Game for Severe Weather

1. **Custom Drinking Game Builder:** Head to [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) and use our custom drinking game builder to create a severe weather pack full of dare challenges, viral drinking games, and meme drinking games.
2. **Deal the Cards:** Shuffle your cards or digital deck.
3. **Assign Roles:** Pick a “Weathercaster”—this person calls out the weather changes and dares.

### Severe Weather Game Play & Drinking Challenges

- **Lightning Strike:** Whenever someone says “lightning,” everyone takes a drink. If you’re last, do a dare.
- **Thunderclap:** When thunder rumbles (or the playlist drops a thunder sound), the Weathercaster draws a card or pulls a challenge. Complete it or face a penalty shot.
- **Hurricane Spin:** Every 15 minutes, everyone must spin around three times, then attempt a simple task (like stacking cups). Fail? Bottoms up!
- **Flood Alert:** Pair up (couples drinking games, anyone?). If the Weathercaster calls “Flood Alert,” pairs must swap drinks and share a dare challenge together.
- **NSFW Dares:** For the truly wild, slip in a few NSFW dares for those who want the night to escalate.
