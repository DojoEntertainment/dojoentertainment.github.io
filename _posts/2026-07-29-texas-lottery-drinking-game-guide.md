## What You Need

- At least 3 friends (the more, the merrier)
- A pile of Texas Lottery scratch-off tickets (or print out some fake ones if you’re outside Texas)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzers, cocktails—your call)
- Dare slips or cards (optional, but highly recommended for extra spice)
- Pens or coins for scratching

## How to Play the Texas Lottery Drinking Game

### Setup

1. Shuffle the scratch-off tickets and place them in a pile.
2. If you’re using dare slips, mix them up in a bowl.
3. Everyone grabs a drink and circles up.

### Game Rules

1. **Draw & Scratch:**  
   On your turn, draw a Texas Lottery scratch-off ticket from the pile. Scratch it off in front of the group.

2. **Win or Drink:**  
   - **If you win anything (even a free ticket):** You get to hand out drinks! The amount you win decides how many sips you give out.  
     - Win $1? Hand out 1 sip.  
     - Win $5? Five sips, split however you want.
   - **If you don’t win:** Take a drink yourself.

3. **Dare Challenge Round:**  
   Every time someone wins $10 or more (or the highest win if you’re using fake tickets), they must draw a dare slip and complete the challenge. This is where you can go wild with NSFW dares or meme drinking games inspired by Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder.

4. **Bonus Round:**  
   - If anyone scratches three losers in a row, they must finish their drink or take on a viral drinking game dare chosen by the group.

5. **Couples Twist:**  
   Playing in pairs? Partners have to split winnings and sips, but if both lose on the same round, they complete a couples dare or a wedding party game challenge.

### Ending the Game

Keep playing until all the scratch-offs are gone, or until the drinks—or the crew—run dry. The player with the most winnings at the end gets crowned the “Lone Star Legend” and can assign a group dare.
