## Weather-Proof Party Drinking Games

San Antonio weather is famously unpredictable. So why not make it the star of your next house party idea? Here’s how to turn every heatwave, thunderstorm, or breezy evening into a viral drinking game you’ll talk about for years.

### 1. The Weather Watcher Challenge

**Perfect for:** Rooftop parties, backyard BBQs, or any night with a view

**What you need:**
- Drinks of your choice
- Access to a weather app or the local news
- Friends ready to get competitive

**How to play:**
1. At the start of the night, everyone predicts the temperature at a set time (for example, 10 PM).
2. Each person writes down their guess.
3. When the time hits, check the actual temperature.
4. Closest guess hands out drinks to two people; the farthest off takes a dare challenge chosen by the group (bonus points for NSFW dares if you’re feeling bold).
5. Throw in a meme drinking game twist: The loser has to post a weather meme in your group chat.

### 2. Storm Chaser Dare Game

**Perfect for:** Those classic Texas thunderstorm nights

**What you need:**
- Drinks
- A deck of dare cards or use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to make your own wild NSFW dares
- Bluetooth speaker for stormy vibes

**How to play:**
1. Every time thunder rolls or lightning flashes, everyone takes a drink.
2. The first person to spot a weather alert on their phone picks someone to complete a dare challenge.
3. If it rains, the whole group does a viral drinking game—like waterfall or flip cup—outside (umbrellas optional).

### 3. Sun’s Out, Fun’s Out

**Perfect for:** Blazing hot San Antonio afternoons

**What you need:**
- Sunglasses for everyone
- Drinks (keep them cold!)
- A list of classic meme drinking games

**How to play:**
1. Every time someone mentions how hot it is, everyone drinks.
2. If anyone takes off their sunglasses, they have to do a dare.
3. Bonus: Try couples drinking games—if you’re with a partner, swap drinks every time you hear the word “humidity.”
