## What Is Knicks?

Knicks isn’t just another party drinking game—it’s a wild mix of memory, mischief, and fast reactions. The secret sauce? Simple rules, zero prep, and the power to turn any group into legends. Whether you’re kicking off a destination wedding, celebrating Election Day, or just vibing with friends, knicks brings the energy.

## What You Need

- Any group of 3+ players (the more, the messier)
- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzers, cocktails, whatever)
- A table or flat surface
- No cards, dice, or fancy props required

## How To Play Knicks

1. **Everyone grabs a drink and circles up.**  
   You can play sitting or standing—whatever fits your vibe.

2. **Pick a starting player.**  
   This is usually the last person who took a drink (or whoever’s boldest).

3. **The starting player taps the table and says “knicks.”**  
   Now, going clockwise, each person must also tap the table and say “knicks.”  

4. **Here’s the twist:**  
   At *any* time, a player can double-tap the table and say “double knicks!” This reverses the direction of play. The next person must react fast and continue in the new direction.

5. **Make a mistake?**  
   If you hesitate, forget to tap, mess up the order, or say the wrong phrase, you drink. (For extra chaos, add a dare challenge from Drinking Dojo’s [custom dare packs](https://drinkingdojo.com).)

6. **Level up with meme drinking game rules:**  
   - Add a “no smiling” rule—smile and you drink.  
   - Assign secret gestures (e.g., scratch your nose before your turn). Miss it? That’s a penalty drink.  
   - Try a round where everyone plays with their non-dominant hand.
