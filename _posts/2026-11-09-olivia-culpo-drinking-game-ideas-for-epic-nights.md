### Setup

1. Gather your group in a circle. Everyone should have a drink in hand.  
2. Open up Olivia Culpo’s Instagram, TikTok, or your fave meme page for inspiration.  
3. Decide if you want to play with cards, or use the Drinking Dojo custom builder to create a game pack with Olivia-themed dares and challenges.  

### Rules

1. **Sip for Style**  
   Every time someone mentions Olivia’s outfits, or anyone is wearing something extra stylish, everyone sips.  

2. **Selfie Challenge**  
   Take a group selfie. If it takes more than three tries to get it right (no closed eyes, no photobombs), everyone drinks. Bonus sip for anyone who posts it and tags Olivia.  

3. **Meme Match**  
   Find a meme that matches Olivia’s energy. If you can’t, take two drinks. The best meme gets to make a rule for the next round.  

4. **Dare Challenge**  
   Use the Drinking Dojo dare challenges or come up with your own Olivia-inspired NSFW dares. Example: “Recreate Olivia’s iconic runway walk” or “Give your best beauty queen wave.” Refuse a dare? Take a shot.  

5. **Trivia Time**  
   Ask an Olivia Culpo-themed trivia question (e.g., “Which pageant did she win?”). Wrong answers drink. Right answers pick someone else to drink.  

6. **Influencer Moment**  
   Anyone who accidentally starts acting like an influencer (posing, talking about followers, etc.) must drink.  

7. **Wildcard Rule**  
   At the start, each player makes up a rule inspired by Olivia’s lifestyle (like “no phones at the table” or “cheers after every round”). Breaking your own rule? Time to drink.
