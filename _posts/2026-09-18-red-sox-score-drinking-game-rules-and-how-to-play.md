## How to Play the Red Sox Score Drinking Game

Crank up the energy every time the Red Sox score (and then some). Here’s how to play:

### Basic Rules

1. **Pre-Game Cheers:** Everyone takes a sip for good luck at first pitch.
2. **Run Rules:**
   - Every time the Red Sox score a run, everyone drinks!
   - If it’s a home run, double the sips—or pick someone for a dare challenge.
3. **Wild Card Plays:**
   - Sox steal a base? Last to shout “Safe!” takes a drink.
   - Double play? Everyone does a quick group toast.
   - Pitching change? Time for a meme drinking game: everyone shares their best Sox meme; funniest one assigns a dare.
4. **Opponent Scores:** If the other team scores, everyone does a “cheer up” shot (or sip if you’re pacing).
5. **7th Inning Stretch:** Quick round of NSFW dares (if your crew is up for it). Not feeling spicy? Go for couples drinking games or a wedding party games twist instead.

## Level Up With Dare Challenges
