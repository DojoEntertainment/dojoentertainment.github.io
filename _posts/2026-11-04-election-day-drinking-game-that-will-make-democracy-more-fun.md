Election night: the tension, the snacks, the endless talking heads—and now, the party drinking game that turns political drama into legendary memories. Whether you’re a political junkie, just in it for the memes, or want an excuse to get wild with friends, the Election Day Drinking Game is your ticket to an unforgettable night. Grab your mates, your favorite drinks, and let’s make democracy a little less dry.

## Why Play an Election Day Drinking Game?

Election Day is a social event, so why not level up your house party ideas with a viral drinking game? From awkward debates to meme-worthy gaffes, politics is the perfect backdrop for some dare challenges, laughter, and bonding over your beverage of choice. Whether you’re in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, or Denmark, these rules will fit right in with your local election coverage.

## What You Need

- A TV, laptop, or phone streaming live election coverage  
- Enough drinks for everyone (cocktails, beer, mocktails—your call)  
- Shot glasses or personal cups  
- Printed or digital list of rules  
- Optional: Scoreboard, snacks, and a meme generator for bonus points

## How To Play: The Rules

You can play solo, in teams, or as a full squad. Assign someone as the “Election Official” to keep track of rules and dares. Here’s your blueprint for mayhem:

### Basic Rules

**Take a sip when:**
- A candidate says “hard-working families,” “change,” or “moving forward”
- Someone mentions “polls” or “swing states”
- The news flashes a map with dramatic music
- There’s an awkward silence after a tough question

**Take two sips when:**
- Someone dodges a question completely  
- An analyst brings up a viral meme or social media trend  
- A technical glitch happens on air  
- You see the host sipping water (or pretending not to laugh)

**Finish your drink or take a shot if:**
- Your country’s election is called for a winner  
- A candidate concedes live on TV  
- Someone cries on air (happy or sad tears count)  
- A major network makes a projection... and gets it wrong

### Dare Challenges

Want to take it up a notch? Toss in some NSFW dares or meme drinking games:
- **Dare Jar:** Everyone writes a dare and drops it in a jar. When a candidate interrupts another, draw a dare and complete it!
- **Meme Challenge:** If a candidate or commentator says something meme-worthy, first person to shout “MEME!” gets to make up a drinking rule for the group.
- **Couples Drinking Games:** Pair up! If you and your partner vote differently on a prediction, loser does a silly dare.

### Bonus House Party Ideas

- Create your own custom drinking game builder [here](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add local election quirks or inside jokes.
- Add themed snacks—red and blue cocktails, flag cookies, or “swing state” sliders.
- Award prizes for best political impression or most epic dare attempt.

## Safety and Social Vibes

Remember, the best drinking games are the ones where everyone wakes up with memories (not regrets). Pace yourself, swap in water rounds, and make sure everyone feels included—no matter their candidate, country, or drink of choice.
