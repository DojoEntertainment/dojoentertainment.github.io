## How To Play: Political Endorsement Edition

### Setup

1. Gather everyone in a circle and hand out drinks.
2. Print or pull up a list of common political phrases (think: “bipartisan effort,” “for the people,” “change we can believe in,” or your favorite meme-worthy slogans).
3. Assign each player a famous politician or party, or let everyone pick their own.

### The Rules

**Drink When:**
- Someone on-screen or in the room drops a classic endorsement catchphrase.
- A politician dodges a question or changes the subject.
- An audience member boos or cheers wildly.
- Someone tries to fact-check in real time.
- You hear a meme-worthy moment (“I’m just here so I won’t get fined,” anyone?).

**Dare Challenges:**
- If your assigned politician or party gets mentioned, you must complete a dare from the Drinking Dojo dare pack.
- Land on an NSFW dare? Double points (and double drinks) if you actually go for it.
- For an extra twist, use Drinking Dojo’s custom drinking game builder to add in wild, personalized dares based on local or viral scandals.

**Bonus Rounds:**  
- If a meme from the event goes viral before the night is over, everyone finishes their drink.
- On election day or inauguration day, play a couples drinking game version—teams of two, last duo standing wins bragging rights (and maybe a hangover cure in the morning).
