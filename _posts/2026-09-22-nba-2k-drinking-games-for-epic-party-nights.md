## NBA 2K Drinking Game Rules

Here’s a no-nonsense guide to get your NBA 2K drinking game rolling. You can play 1v1, team up in twos, or rotate players tournament-style. Each rule below triggers a drink or a dare!

### Basic Game Rules

- **Missed Free Throw:** Take 1 sip  
- **Turnover (bad pass, out of bounds, etc.):** Take 2 sips  
- **Blocked Shot:** Player whose shot was blocked takes 2 sips  
- **Three-Pointer Made:** Everyone but the shooter takes 1 sip  
- **And-One Play:** Loser of the play takes 1 sip, shooter assigns a dare challenge  
- **Foul Out:** Finish your drink  

### Dare Challenges

Spice up your NBA 2K drinking games with party dares. Every time you hit one of these milestones, pull a card from your dare deck (or use Drinking Dojo’s online [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)):

- **Double-Double Achieved:** Assign a dare to another player  
- **Alley-Oop Dunk:** Everyone but the dunker must complete an NSFW dare (if you dare)  
- **Lose by 20+ Points:** The loser must take on a viral meme drinking challenge
