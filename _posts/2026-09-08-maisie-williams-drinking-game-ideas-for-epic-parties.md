## Maisie Williams Meme Drinking Game

This party drinking game gets everyone hyped with classic Maisie moments. Perfect for house parties, chill nights, or even as one of your wedding party games.

### Rules

1. **Watch a Maisie Williams Movie or TV Show Clip**  
   Pick a few iconic scenes from Game of Thrones, interviews, or any of her viral videos.

2. **Drink When...**
   - Someone says “Arya” (or any GoT reference)
   - Maisie cracks a joke or makes a meme face
   - She drops an epic clapback
   - You spot a viral meme featuring Maisie

3. **Dare Challenge Round**
   - Every time someone in the group laughs out loud, they must draw a dare card.  
   - Dares can be NSFW dares or safe-for-work—your call!

4. **Wild Card**
   - If someone can quote Maisie perfectly, everyone else drinks.
