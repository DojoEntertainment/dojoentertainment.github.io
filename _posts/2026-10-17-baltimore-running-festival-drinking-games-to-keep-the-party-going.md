## Epic Baltimore Running Festival Drinking Games

### 1. Mile Marker Madness

**Materials Needed:**  
- Your favorite drinks  
- A marker (can be a sign, cone, or just your phone)  
- Dare cards (make your own or grab a [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) pack)

**How to Play:**  
- Pick a spot along the course (mile markers, water stations, or finish line hype zones).  
- Each time a runner you know passes, everyone watching takes a sip.  
- If your runner does a victory dance or weird move, the group does a dare challenge—draw from your dare cards or create your own epic NSFW dares.  
- Last person to spot your runner chugs!

### 2. Runner or Meme

**Materials Needed:**  
- A phone with meme images  
- Drinks (duh)  
- Meme drinking games deck (optional but highly encouraged)

**How to Play:**  
- Before the festival, collect the wildest running memes.  
- When someone spots a runner whose outfit or style matches a meme, shout it out.  
- Everyone votes on the resemblance. If you’re the only one who sees it, you drink.  
- Every time you find a spot-on match, assign a drinking challenge to anyone in your crew.

### 3. Finish Line Dare Challenges

**Materials Needed:**  
- Drinks  
- Dare list (include some spicy NSFW dares if your group is up for it)  
- Pennies or tokens

**How to Play:**  
- Each time a friend crosses the finish line, the group draws a dare.  
- Runner gets to assign the dare to anyone or choose to complete it themselves.  
- If nobody’s brave enough, everyone drinks!  
- Bonus: For wedding party games or couples drinking games, add a romantic twist to the dare list.
