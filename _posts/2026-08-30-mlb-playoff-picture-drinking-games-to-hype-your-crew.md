## How To Play: MLB Playoff Picture Drinking Game

**Set up:**  
Gather your squad, crack open your drinks, and get the playoff broadcast rolling. Decide if you want to play solo, in pairs (great for couples drinking games), or break into teams.

**Rules:**

### Basic Plays

- **Strikeout:** Everyone takes one sip.  
- **Home Run:** Last person to raise their glass finishes their drink.  
- **Double Play:** Pick a partner—losing team does a dare.  
- **Pitching Change:** Everyone switches drinks (or seats).  

### Dare Challenges

Spice it up with dare challenges when something juicy happens:

- **Meme Moment:** When the camera catches a wild fan or awkward mascot, the group must come up with a meme caption. Best one assigns a dare.  
- **Umpire Meltdown:** If a manager argues with the ump, everyone does a group toast and the loudest person shouts their favorite team chant.  
- **Ninth Inning Nerves:** Every run scored in the ninth inning doubles your next sip.
