## Game Setup for the Ultimate Party Drinking Games Experience

1. **Gather your crew:** This game works best with 3–10 players.
2. **Pick your drinks:** Beer for the classic feel, cocktails for extra chaos, or non-alcoholic options if you’re keeping it chill.
3. **Create your dare deck:** Use pre-made cards or design your own dares with a custom drinking game builder.
4. **Turn on the DP World Tour:** You don’t need to know every rule—just enjoy the action!

## How to Play the DP World Tour Drinking Game with Dare Challenges

**Every player picks a favorite golfer** (or gets assigned one randomly).

### Drink When:

- Your golfer gets a birdie (1 drink)
- Anyone lands in a bunker (everyone drinks)
- Announcer says “pressure” (1 drink)
- Someone misses a short putt (2 drinks)
- There’s a water hazard (finish your drink if it’s your golfer)

### Dare Challenges and NSFW Dares

After every three holes, the group draws a dare card. These can get as wild or NSFW as your squad wants (think: “Speak in a British accent until your next turn” or “Share your wildest meme-worthy story”).

### Viral Meme Drinking Games Moments

When a player spots a viral meme reference (like a golfer’s wild outfit or unexpected celebration), call it out! First to spot it gives out 2 drinks.

### Extra Drinking Challenges for House Party Ideas

Not enough action? Add these:

- **Longest Drive:** Everyone tries to hit a ping pong ball with a spoon. Farthest wins, losers drink.
- **Putt Off:** Set up a mini putting challenge—miss and you take a dare.

## Winning and Custom House Rules for Couples Drinking Games & Wedding Party Games

- Player with the most birdies (or successful dares completed) at the end wins bragging rights—and maybe a custom dare for next time.
- Make it extra wild by adding your own house party ideas, like assigning different drinks for double bogeys or creating couples drinking games twists for date night.
