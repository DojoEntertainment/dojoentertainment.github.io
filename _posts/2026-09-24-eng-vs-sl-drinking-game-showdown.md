## How to Play Eng vs SL Drinking Game

### 1. **Team Up**
- Divide the crew into Team Eng and Team SL. Jerseys, face paint, and accents are 100% encouraged.

### 2. **Game Start Ritual**
- Everyone does a group cheers and takes a starter sip to set the mood.

### 3. **Match Event Triggers**
Assign drinks and dares based on match events. Here are some classic triggers:
- **Boundary (4 or 6 scored)**: The opposing team takes 1 drink.
- **Wicket falls**: Everyone on the losing team must pull a dare card.
- **Century or Fifty**: Everyone drinks in honor of the player.
- **Dropped catch or misfield**: The guilty team takes 2 drinks each.
- **Sledging or funny moment**: Quick meme challenge—first to meme the moment picks someone to drink.

### 4. **Dare Challenges**
- Keep the energy high with dare challenges from Drinking Dojo’s wild packs or your own custom dares. Think NSFW dares, meme dares, and anything that’ll get the party laughing.
- Example dares: 
  - "Do your best commentator impression"
  - "Make a toast in a British or Sri Lankan accent"
  - "Swap seats with someone on the rival team until the next wicket"

### 5. **Halftime/Breaks**
- Use breaks for bonus challenges like:
  - Quick-fire meme drinking games (caption that highlight, loser drinks)
  - House party ideas like team trivia or couple’s dares if you’re partying with a significant other

### 6. **Victory Lap**
- Winning team does a group photo (bonus points for funny poses) and the losing team has to perform a dare chosen by the winners.
