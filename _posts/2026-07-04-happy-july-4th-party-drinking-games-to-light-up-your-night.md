## 1. Stars and Shots: Patriotic Drinking Game for July 4th

This one’s all about quick reflexes and American pride (plus a little friendly chaos).

**What you need:**  
- Red, white, and blue solo cups  
- Ping pong balls  
- Your drink of choice

**How to play:**  
1. Split everyone into two teams.  
2. Arrange the cups in a star shape on each side of a table.  
3. Teams take turns bouncing ping pong balls into the other team’s cups.  
4. When a ball lands in a cup, the other team must drink, then remove the cup.  
5. Last team with cups on their side standing wins.

**Pro tip:** Losers take on a dare challenge from the Drinking Dojo [dare pack](https://drinkingdojo.com).

## 2. Meme Drinking Game: Viral Meme Drinking Challenges

What’s more American than memes? Get the laughs and the drinks flowing.

**What you need:**  
- A stack of printed memes (or a phone/tablet for digital memes)  
- Drinks

**How to play:**  
1. Each player draws a meme card or scrolls to a random meme.  
2. Read it out loud and assign it to someone in the group who fits the meme best.  
3. That person takes a sip (or a shot, if you’re feeling bold).  
4. Keep it going until everyone’s had a turn—or until you run out of memes.

## 4. Wedding Party Games With a Patriotic Twist: Wedding Drinking Games for July 4th Celebrations

Got a July wedding or just want to practice your bouquet toss? Spice up the party with these wedding-inspired drinking games.

**What you need:**  
- A bouquet (real or fake)  
- Drinks

**How to play:**  
1. Form a circle and play music.  
2. Pass the bouquet around; when the music stops, whoever’s holding it must do a dare or take a shot.  
3. Bonus: The person who ends up with the bouquet at the end leads the next round with a dare of their choice.

## 5. House Party Ideas: Independence Day Edition Drinking Games

If you’re hosting, set up a rotation of drinking games throughout the night:

- **Fortnite Drinking Game:** Drop in, take a sip for every elimination, and do a shot if you win a Victory Royale.  
- **Couples Drinking Games:** Pair up and test your knowledge of each other with trivia—wrong answers = drinks.  
- **Drinking Challenges:** Set up relay races, flip cup, or beer pong tournaments. Winner gets bragging rights (and maybe a custom dare for the losers).
