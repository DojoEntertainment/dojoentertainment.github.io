## Top Drinking Games for Time Change 2026

### 1. **The Hourly Dare Challenge**

*Perfect for: House parties, dorm rooms, and anyone who refuses to waste a single second.*

#### What You Need:
- A deck of cards (or use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com))
- List of dares (meme-inspired, NSFW, or classic—your call)
- Drinks of your choice

#### How to Play:
1. Set an alarm for every hour (or half-hour if you’re feeling bold).
2. When the alarm goes off, everyone draws a card.
3. Each card matches a dare from your list. For example:
   - Red cards = meme drinking games dares
   - Black cards = viral drinking challenges
4. Complete the dare or take a drink!

#### Pro Tip: Add themed dares based on the time change—like “Pretend you’re stuck in a time loop until your next turn.”

### 2. **Spring Forward, Fall Back Sips**

*Perfect for: Wedding party games, couples drinking games, or chill nights with friends.*

#### What You Need:
- Two different colored cups or shot glasses
- Drinks (beer, cocktails, or whatever you vibe with)
- Timer or phone

#### How to Play:
1. Each player picks a cup: one for "Spring Forward" and one for "Fall Back."
2. Set the timer—every time it buzzes, flip a coin.
   - Heads: Everyone with a "Spring Forward" cup drinks.
   - Tails: "Fall Back" players drink.
3. Random dares can be tossed in for anyone who forgets which cup is which!

#### Pro Tip: Make it extra spicy by adding NSFW dares for anyone who picks up the wrong cup.

### 3. **Time Traveler Tag**

*Perfect for: Big groups, house party ideas, and anyone who loves a bit of chaos.*

#### What You Need:
- Sticky notes
- Pens
- Drinks

#### How to Play:
1. Write different years on sticky notes (like 1999, 2026, or your favorite Fortnite drinking game year).
2. Everyone sticks a year to their forehead.
3. If you call someone by their real name instead of their “year,” you drink.
4. The host throws out random dare challenges—like “All 1999s do a meme dance!”
