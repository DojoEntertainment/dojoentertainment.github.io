### Classic NFL Scores Sips Rules

- **Every Field Goal:** Take one sip.

- **Two-Point Conversion:** Last person to raise their drink does a dare challenge.
- **Interception:** Pick a player to finish their drink (or take a big gulp).
- **Missed Extra Point:** Everyone groans and takes a sip.
