## Game 1: Bridge Bash Dare Challenge

**Perfect for:** Big groups, house parties, or outdoor celebrations

### How to Play

1. **Set Up:** Everyone sits or stands in a circle. Place a stack of cards (or slips of paper) in the center, each with a dare or meme challenge inspired by bridges, Ben Franklin, or local legends.
2. **Go Around:** The first player draws a card and must either complete the dare or take a drink.
3. **Pass It On:** If someone refuses a dare, they take two drinks and pick a new player to try the same challenge.
4. **Keep It Going:** Go around the circle until everyone’s had a turn—or until the pack runs out!

**Dare ideas:**
- Give your best Ben Franklin impersonation (bonus if you use a meme filter!)
- Recite a fun fact about the Ben Franklin Bridge. If you can’t, drink twice.
- Make up a bridge-related toast and have everyone cheers.
- Snap a group selfie and post with a ridiculous hashtag.
- NSFW dares (optional): swap stories about your wildest party moment or reveal your secret party skill.

## Game 2: Bridge Trivia Buzz

**Perfect for:** Trivia lovers, couples drinking games, or smaller groups

### How to Play

1. **Trivia Time:** Nominate a quizmaster. They’ll ask Ben Franklin Bridge or Philly-related questions (Google is your friend!).
2. **Answer Fast:** Players buzz in or shout out answers. First correct answer gives out a drink; wrong answer takes a drink.
3. **Dare Round:** Every third question, the lowest scorer must pull a random dare from the Dare Challenge pile.

## Game 3: Bridge Crossing Relay

**Perfect for:** Outdoor parties, wedding party games, or festival vibes

### How to Play

1. **Teams:** Split into two teams. Set up a “bridge” (blanket, plank, or a taped line).
2. **Relay Race:** Each team member must cross the “bridge” while balancing a drink. Drop it, and your team drinks!
3. **Challenge Rounds:** Add in dare challenges halfway across—like telling a meme-worthy joke or completing a quick dance move.
4. **First to Finish:** Winning team gets to assign drinks, losing team completes a group dare.
