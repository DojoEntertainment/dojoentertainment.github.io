## What Are Flights in Drinking Games?

No, we’re not talking about hopping on a plane—flights are all about tasting, ranking, and laughing your way through a lineup of different drinks. Think of it as a tasting adventure, but with a side of dare challenges, meme-worthy moments, and a sprinkle of mayhem. This trend is blowing up across house parties and viral drinking games everywhere, and for good reason: it's fun, easy to play, and totally customizable.

## What You’ll Need

- **A selection of drinks** (beer, cider, cocktails, seltzers, or even themed drinks—get creative!)
- **Small tasting glasses or shot glasses** (aim for 4–6 per player)
- **Scorecards or your phone’s notes app**
- **Custom drinking game builder** (optional, but way more fun—check out [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) for packs and tools)
- **Dare cards or a list of NSFW dares** (if you want to up the chaos)

## How to Play Flights The Drinking Game Way

### 1. Set Up Your Flight

Pick 4–6 different drinks. They can all be beers, ciders, spirits, or a wild mix—it’s your flight, your rules. Pour a small amount of each into separate tasting glasses for every player.

### 2. Assign Numbers

Number each drink (1 to however many you have). Keep the bottles hidden if you want to make it a guessing game.

### 3. Taste and Rate

Take turns tasting each drink. After each round, rate them on flavour, strength, or “most likely to turn you into a meme tomorrow.” Keep notes or scorecards handy.

### 4. Guess the Drink

Once you’ve tasted them all, see if anyone can guess what’s in each glass. The player with the most correct guesses wins… or maybe they just don’t have to do the dare challenge.

### 5. Dare Challenges

Here’s where the party drinking game magic happens. After each round, losers (lowest scores or worst guesses) draw a dare card. You can go classic, NSFW dares, or even meme drinking games—whatever fits your vibe. Looking for inspiration? The [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) at Drinking Dojo is your secret weapon.

### 6. Make It Viral

Snap pics, make videos, and turn your flight into a viral drinking game moment. Bonus points for the best reaction faces or wildest dares completed.
