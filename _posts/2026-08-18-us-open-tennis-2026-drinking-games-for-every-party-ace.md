## How to Play: US Open Tennis 2026 Drinking Games

### Game 1: Serve & Sip

**Perfect for:** Big groups, casual fans, house parties

**Rules:**
- Every time a player serves an ace, everyone takes a sip.
- Double fault? Last person to shout “Let!” does a dare challenge.
- If the commentators mention a celebrity in the crowd, everyone drinks.
- When a player argues with the umpire, the group picks someone to finish their drink.

### Game 2: Tie-Breaker Dare

**Perfect for:** Couples drinking games, small gatherings

**Rules:**
- Each player chooses a side of the court (left or right).
- Every time a point is won on your side, you give out a sip.
- During a tie-break, losers must complete NSFW dares from your custom pack.
- If the match goes to five sets, everyone takes a group shot.

### Game 3: Meme Match

**Perfect for:** Meme lovers, social media fiends

**Rules:**
- Every time a meme-worthy moment happens (awkward celebration, wild outfit, camera close-up), the group pauses and creates a meme.
- The best meme (judged by laughter or online votes) gets to assign a dare challenge.
- If your meme goes viral (gets 10+ likes in your group chat), everyone else drinks.
