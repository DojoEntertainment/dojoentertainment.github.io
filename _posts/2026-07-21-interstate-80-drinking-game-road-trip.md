Ever been on a road trip with your crew, cruising down Interstate 80, and thought, “This would be way more epic with some party drinking games?” You’re not alone. Whether you’re chilling at a motel in Omaha, catching sunsets in Salt Lake City, or celebrating finishing a long drive, the Interstate 80 Drinking Game is about to turn your travels into legendary memories. 

## What You Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzer, cocktails—you do you)
- At least 2 players (the more, the rowdier)
- A playlist of road trip bangers
- Dare challenges or a custom drinking game builder (check out [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) for viral drinking games and meme drinking games)
- A map or GPS app (for those bonus location-based dares)

## How To Play

The best part? You can play this drinking game at your pregame stop, roadside motel, or once you’ve parked for the night (seriously, don’t play and drive). Here’s how to get the party started:

### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Route**  
   Everyone picks a city, town, or rest stop along Interstate 80. Whenever you pass by or talk about someone’s pick, they drink.

2. **License Plate Bingo**  
   Spot a plate from a new state? The first person to call it out picks someone to take a drink. If it’s from one of the I-80 states (think Ohio, Nebraska, Nevada), everyone drinks.

3. **Roadside Dare Challenges**  
   Every time you see a classic roadside sign (think “World’s Largest...” or “Historic Landmark”), stop and draw a dare from your meme drinking games pack. NSFW dares? That’s up to your squad.

4. **Viral Moments**  
   Someone spots a viral meme on a billboard, truck, or at a rest stop? Instant group drink and a silly dare from the Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder.

5. **Bonus Rounds**  
   - **Wedding Party Games:** If you spot a wedding venue, the group plays a quick wedding party mini-game (toast, roast, or dare).
   - **Couples Drinking Games:** Couples in the car? Pair up for couples drinking game rounds. Last pair standing gets bragging rights.
   - **House Party Ideas:** End the day at your hotel with a house party theme—pick a pack from Drinking Dojo and keep the night alive.

## Game Variations

- **Election Day Drinking Game:** If you’re on the road during an election, add bonus drinks for every campaign sign.
- **Fortnite Drinking Game:** Spot a Fortnite sticker or shirt? Outrageous Fortnite dare for the group.

## Safety Tips

- Never play while driving. Wait until you’re parked for the night or chilling at your stop.
- Hydrate and snack up—no one likes a hangover on the road.
- Respect local laws and everyone’s limits.
