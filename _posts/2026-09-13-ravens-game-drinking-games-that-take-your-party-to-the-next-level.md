## Ravens Game Drinking Game Rules

Ready to turn your next Ravens game into a party highlight? Here’s how to play one of our favorite Ravens game drinking games.

### What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzer, cocktails — you do you)
- A group of friends (2–10 is perfect)
- TV/streaming setup for the Ravens game
- Scorecards (optional, but fun for keeping track)
- Dare challenges and meme cards (grab a pack from [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) for max chaos)

### How to Play

**Basic Rules:**
1. **Choose Your Drinks:** Everyone picks their drink of choice.
2. **Set Dare Challenges:** Shuffle your dare/meme cards and place them in a pile.
3. **Kickoff Toast:** Everyone raises a glass as the game starts. Cheers to wild times!
4. **Take a Sip When:**
    - The Ravens score a touchdown.
    - The commentators mention “defense” or “Lamar Jackson.”
    - There’s a flag on the play.
    - Someone in the room yells at the ref or the TV (let’s be honest, it’ll happen).
5. **Dare Challenges:**
    - When the Ravens make a comeback or a big play, draw a dare card. Complete the dare or finish your drink.
    - NSFW dares? Optional, but definitely spice up the vibes if your crew is game.
6. **Chug or Shot Round:**
    - If the Ravens win, everyone toasts and takes a shot.
    - If they lose, the biggest fan in the room does a viral dare challenge (think TikTok-style — or invent your own with our custom drinking game builder).

### Bonus House Rules

- **Meme Drinking Games:** Share your best Ravens-related memes between quarters — best meme wins a pass on the next dare.
- **Penalty Shot:** False start in the room (someone spills or knocks over a drink)? Penalty shot or chug!
- **Double Down:** Call the next touchdown scorer. If you’re right, hand out drinks. Wrong? Drink double.
