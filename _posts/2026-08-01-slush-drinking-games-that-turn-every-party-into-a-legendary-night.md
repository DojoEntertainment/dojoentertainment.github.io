## What Is Slush and Why Is It Viral?

Slush is more than just a frosty drink—it’s a party drinking game where every round is a new adventure. Think classic card games, but with a twist of wild dares, meme drinking games, and custom rule chaos. All you need is a group of fun-loving friends, some icy slushies (alcoholic or not), and a willingness to embrace the mayhem.

## What You Need to Play Slush

- **Blended slush drinks** (your choice of flavors and spirits)  
- **Plastic cups or mason jars**  
- **A pack of cards or a custom drinking game builder** (like the ones at [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com))  
- **Printed or digital dare challenges**  
- **Music playlist for party vibes**  
- **A sense of humor**  

## How to Play Slush Drinking Game

### Setup

1. **Fill everyone’s cup with their chosen slush drink.**  
2. **Shuffle your pack of cards or load up your custom drinking game builder.**  
3. **Set out your dare challenges or meme-based cards.**  
4. **Choose a game master** (someone to keep the chaos under control).  

### Basic Rules

- Players sit in a circle, drinks in hand.  
- Each player draws a card on their turn.  
- The card determines their fate:  
    - **Number cards:** Correspond to classic actions (e.g., 2 = choose two people to drink, 7 = heaven—hands up, last one drinks).  
    - **Face cards:** Trigger a dare challenge or meme drinking game round. Get ready for NSFW dares, viral TikTok moves, or hilarious forfeits.  
    - **Aces:** Everyone takes a big slush sip.  
- If you can’t complete a dare, finish your drink or create a new house rule that sticks for the rest of the game.  

### Dare Challenges and Meme Drinking Game Ideas

- **Fortnite drinking game round:** Last person to do a Fortnite dance chugs their slush.  
- **Election day drinking game round:** Assign political-themed dares—impressions, speeches, or debate-style roast battles.  
- **Wedding party games twist:** Pair up as “newlyweds” and answer funny relationship questions. Wrong answers mean both drink!  
- **NSFW dares:** Only for the brave—pull a dare from the adult pile and hope for mercy.  
- **Couples drinking games:** Double dare for pairs, with the option to swap drinks or secrets.  

### Pro Tips

- Make your own custom slush rules using a drinking game builder. Add inside jokes, regional slang, or wild meme references.  
- Use frozen fruit or candy as garnishes for bonus rounds (eat one, take a shot).  
- If someone spills their slush, they have to do a dare chosen by the group.
