## How To Play

Get your squad together—this is perfect for house parties, chill nights, or even as wedding party games for your tennis-loving friends. Here’s how it works:

### Before the Match

1. **Pick Your Player**  
   Everyone picks a tennis player to root for (bonus points if it’s Rublev, obviously).  
2. **Set Up Your Drinks**  
   Make sure everyone’s glass is full. No one wants to run dry mid-rally.

### During the Match

Every time the following happens, follow the dare challenge below:

- **Rublev Wins a Point:**  
  *Everyone takes a sip.*  
- **Rublev Smashes a Racket:**  
  *Last person to shout “Smash!” takes a double sip.*  
- **Ace Serve by Rublev:**  
  *Hand out a dare to someone else (cue NSFW dares if you’re feeling bold).*  
- **Rublev Argues with the Umpire:**  
  *Everyone must do a meme-worthy impression of a tennis tantrum. Best one gives out two drinks.*  
- **Rublev Wins the Set:**  
  *Finish your drink or take a shot (house rules apply).*

### Extra Dare Challenges

- If Rublev pulls off a wild trick shot, everyone must attempt their best “tennis move” and the funniest performance gives out a drink.  
- Someone mentions another tennis star? That person takes an extra sip.
