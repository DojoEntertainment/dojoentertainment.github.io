## Must-Try Daylight Savings Drinking Games

Ready for the main event? Here are the best drinking games to celebrate daylight savings 2026, including a custom twist you can build yourself.

### 1. The Time Traveler 

**How to Play:**  
- Everyone sets their phone alarms for the daylight savings switch time.  
- Whenever someone’s alarm goes off (thanks, time zones and phone glitches), everyone takes a drink.  
- If two alarms go off at the *exact* same minute, last person to drink does a dare challenge.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Smartphones  
- Drinks of choice  
- Dare cards or the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)

### 2. Lost Hour, Found Shot

**How to Play:**  
- Each player picks a “forbidden word” for the night (think: “clock,” “time,” “sleep”).  
- Any time someone slips up and says a forbidden word, they take a shot.  
- At the official daylight savings moment, everyone finishes their drink and pulls a meme drinking game card.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Shot glasses  
- Drinks  
- Pen and paper  
- Meme drinking games cards (Drinking Dojo’s packs are perfect for this)

### 3. Reverse Happy Hour

**How to Play:**  
- Start a countdown an hour before the switch.  
- Every 10 minutes, play a mini drinking challenge (think: flip cup, dare roulette, or NSFW dares if you’re feeling bold).  
- When the clock changes, everyone teams up for a final group dare.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Timer or clock  
- Drinks  
- Drinking challenge cards  
- Dare cards
