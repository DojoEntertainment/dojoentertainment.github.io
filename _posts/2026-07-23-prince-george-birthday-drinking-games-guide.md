- A deck of cards or your phone for rules
- Some classic memes or photos of Prince George for inspiration
- Your favorite Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder or party pack
- Friends ready for NSFW dares and hilarious drinking challenges

## Game 1: The Royal Sip Off

A classic party drinking game with a royal twist.

**Rules:**
1. Every time someone mentions the words “royal,” “birthday,” or “George,” everyone takes a sip.
2. If a player accidentally calls Prince George by another royal name (like Harry or William), they take a shot.
3. Whenever a meme or photo of Prince George gets shown, everyone drinks — last person to react does a dare.

**Dare Challenges:**  
Keep a stack of dare cards handy. If anyone messes up a rule, draw a dare. Go wild — from NSFW dares to meme-inspired antics.

## Game 2: Prince George Meme Roulette

Time to get creative with some viral drinking games.

**How to Play:**
- Each round, one person finds a new Prince George meme (or makes one on their phone).
- Show the meme to the group. Anyone who laughs takes a drink.
- If no one laughs, the meme creator drinks.
- The best meme of the night (voted by the group) gets to nominate someone for a wild dare.

## Game 3: Royal Dare Challenges

Perfect for couples drinking games or big squads.

**Set Up:**
- Use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) at Drinking Dojo to create a set of dares themed around royal life.
- Examples: “Speak in a posh accent until your next turn,” “Curtsy every time you drink,” or “Tell your most regal secret.”

**How it Works:**
- Everyone draws a dare card on their turn.
- Complete it or drink twice!

- **Snap pics:** Meme them later for the next round of drinking challenges.
