## The Classic USA Network Drinking Game Rules and How to Play

### Step 1: Pick Your Show  
Choose a classic USA Network series everyone knows or wants to discover. *Suits* is a go-to for slick lawyer drama, but *Psych* or *White Collar* are hilarious options too.

### Step 2: Set the Drinking Game Rules  
Here are some party drinking game rules to get you started (feel free to add your own custom twists using our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com)):

- **Take a Sip** every time:
  - Someone says “objection!”
  - There’s a dramatic pause before a big reveal
  - Someone gets caught in a lie
  - The camera zooms in for effect

- **Take Two Sips** if:
  - Someone mentions Harvard (*Suits* fans, you know)
  - A character breaks the law (bonus if it’s in *White Collar*)
  - Pop culture references go over your head

- **Finish Your Drink** if:
  - Someone goes undercover
  - There’s a plot twist nobody saw coming
  - You laugh out loud at a meme-worthy moment

### Step 3: Add Dare Challenges  
Spice things up by throwing in some wild dare challenges from the Drinking Dojo dare pack. If someone breaks a rule or refuses a drink, they pick a dare—think NSFW dares for the brave, or meme challenges for laughs.

### Step 4: Optional Creative Twists  
Try a *couples drinking game* format if you’re double dating, or make it a wedding party game by adding a rule for every time someone says “I do” (hint: *Suits* wedding episodes!).
