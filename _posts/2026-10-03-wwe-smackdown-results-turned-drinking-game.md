### Game Setup

1. Gather your crew (2+ players recommended).
2. Queue up the latest WWE Smackdown episode or highlights featuring those jaw-dropping results.
3. Pour everyone a drink.
4. Pick a wrestler to ‘represent’ each player—when your wrestler is on screen, get ready.

### WWE Smackdown Drinking Game Rules

**Take one sip if:**
- A wrestler taunts the crowd  
- Someone gets thrown over the top rope  
- There’s a surprise entrance  
- The announcer shouts “What a move!” or any classic catchphrase

**Take two sips if:**
- A finishing move lands  
- There’s a controversial pin  
- A manager interferes  
- Someone grabs a chair or another prop

**Finish your drink if:**
- Your chosen wrestler wins a match  
- A wrestler gets disqualified  
- An absolute meme-worthy moment goes viral (think wardrobe malfunctions or wild crowd reactions)

**Bonus Dare Challenges:**
- Every time a wrestler cuts a promo, the group votes on who must do an **NSFW dare** (keep it friendly, keep it legal!)
- If your wrestler loses, you have to pull a dare card or make a toast in your best wrestling persona voice
