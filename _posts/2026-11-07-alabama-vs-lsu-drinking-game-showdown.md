## How to Play the Alabama vs LSU Drinking Game

Ready to turn every snap into a sip? Here’s how you make game day unforgettable:

### Basic Rules

1. **Split into Teams**  
   Group up based on your allegiance—Team Alabama or Team LSU. Neutral fans? Pick a side or sip with both!

2. **Sip for Scores**  
   - *Touchdown*: Everyone on the scoring team takes two drinks.  
   - *Field Goal*: One drink for everyone on the scoring team.  
   - *Turnover*: The team that turned it over does a dare challenge.  
   - *Penalty*: Everyone drinks for five seconds.  

3. **Meme Drinking Triggers**  
   - If the broadcasters mention “rivalry,” everyone takes a sip.  
   - If there’s a crazy fan on camera (painted faces, wild costumes), last one to notice drinks.  
   - Any meme-worthy moment? Vote on it—loser drinks.  

4. **Dare Challenges**  
   Before the game, write out some NSFW dares or classic party dares, and toss them in a bowl. Whenever a team misses a field goal or fumbles, someone from that team has to pull and do a dare.  

5. **Halftime Challenge**  
   At halftime, play a quick custom drinking game round—with couples drinking games or wedding party games themes if you’re feeling extra spicy. Use the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to whip up something epic.  

### Optional House Rules

- *Fortnite Celebration*: If a player does a Fortnite dance after a touchdown on TV, everyone finishes their drink.  
- *Overtime Mayhem*: If the game goes to overtime, everyone draws a dare and completes it before the end of the game.
