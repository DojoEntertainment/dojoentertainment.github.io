## Top Election Day Drinking Games for 2026

### 1. **Candidate Call-Outs**

Every time a candidate’s name is mentioned, take a sip. Simple, savage, and guaranteed to keep you on your toes as the results roll in.

**How to Play:**
- Everyone picks a candidate at the start of the night.
- When your candidate is mentioned (on TV, in a meme, or by someone else), you drink.
- Bonus: If your candidate wins a state or region, everyone else takes two sips.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Drinks of choice  
- TV or livestream of election coverage  
- Score sheet for tracking wins (optional)

### 2. **Election Meme Drinking Game**

Memes and election nights go hand-in-hand. This viral drinking game is all about catching those moments that instantly go trending.

**How to Play:**
- Assign popular meme formats (think “Distracted Boyfriend” or “Woman Yelling at Cat”) to different events (e.g., awkward pauses, outrageous claims, unexpected wins).
- When a meme-worthy moment happens, the group votes on which meme fits best.
- The person who nominates the winning meme gives out a dare.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Drinks  
- Access to Twitter/X, Instagram, or TikTok for meme inspo  
- Dare cards (or use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to make your own dares)

### 3. **NSFW Debate Dares**

For those who like their drinking games spicy, add an NSFW twist. Every time a politician dodges a question or gives a non-answer, draw a dare challenge.

**How to Play:**
- Whenever a candidate dodges or spins a question, pull a dare from the deck.
- Dares can be as wild or as tame as you want—think “text your ex,” “swap an item of clothing,” or “do a dramatic reading of the latest campaign speech.”
- For couples drinking games, add a romantic or cheeky twist to the dares.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Drinks  
- Pre-written NSFW dare cards  
- Timer (for timed dares)

### 4. **Election Bingo Drinking Challenge**

Classic, but with a twist. Each player gets a bingo card filled with election cliches, wild predictions, and viral moments.

**How to Play:**
- Cross off squares as the night goes on. Every time you complete a row or column, you get to make a rule for the group (e.g., “No saying the word ‘vote’ for the next 10 minutes”).
- If you get a blackout, everyone else finishes their drink.

**Materials Needed:**  
- Printable bingo cards (make your own or grab a template at [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com))  
- Drinks  
- Pens/markers
