Election day doesn’t have to be all stress and scrolling through endless updates—why not turn it into the ultimate party drinking game? Whether you're a political junkie, a meme lord, or just here for the dare challenges, this is your guide to making election night unforgettable. Gather your mates, stock the fridge, and get ready for a round of viral drinking games with a ballot box twist.

## What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, cider, cocktails, or mocktails—no judgment here)  
- A TV or device streaming election coverage  
- Cups and shot glasses  
- Printed or digital list of rules  
- Optional: red, blue, or themed party decorations  
- A sense of humor and a competitive spirit  

## Setting Up for the Election Day Drinking Game

Invite your friends over (or connect virtually), set up the space with snacks and drinks, and pick your party drinking games playlist. This election day drinking game works perfectly for house parties, dorm room hangouts, or even a wedding after-party if you really want to get wild.

## How to Play

Each player picks a candidate or party to support (bonus points for themed outfits). Whenever the news coverage hits one of the triggers below, it’s time to drink. The game is flexible—use your own rules or try out the custom drinking game builder at [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add some meme magic or NSFW dares.

### Basic Rules

Take a sip or a shot when:

- A news anchor says “too close to call”  
- A state or region is declared for a candidate  
- Someone on-screen says “historic turnout”  
- The vote count ticker updates dramatically  
- A candidate tweets something meme-worthy (cue the meme drinking games)  
- An election map changes color  

Finish your drink if:

- Your candidate wins (or loses, if you’re feeling spicy)  
- There’s a recount announced  
- Someone mentions “hanging chads” (throwback alert)  
- A celebrity makes a surprise appearance on coverage  

### Dare Challenges

Want to up the ante? Add dare challenges for extra chaos:

- Post a funny meme in your group chat every time you drink  
- Do your best impression of a politician on the next drink  
- Share a wild election prediction—if you’re wrong by the end of the night, finish your drink  

### Optional: NSFW Dares

For a more adult twist, throw in some NSFW dares (consent is key!). Try “Text your ex who they voted for” or “Share your spiciest political meme.”

## Game Variations

- **Couples drinking games:** Pair up and double dare each other when your party’s candidate makes a surprise comeback.  
- **House party ideas:** Turn your living room into a “polling station” with themed zones and meme drinking games at every corner.  

## Safety First

Election day drinking games are all about fun, but know your limits. Drink water, pace yourself, and always look out for your friends—no one wants to end the night in a recount with their dignity.
