## How To Play Sweep | Step-by-Step Drinking Challenges

**Setup:**

1. Shuffle the deck and deal all cards face down evenly among players.
2. Each player keeps their stack face down in front of them—no peeking!

**Basic Gameplay:**

1. Everyone flips the top card of their stack at the same time and places it face up in the center.
2. If two or more players flip the same card value (regardless of suit), it’s a SWEEP!
3. The players involved in the sweep race to slap the pile of matching cards first. The loser (slowest to slap) faces a dare challenge or takes a drink (your group decides the penalty before starting).
4. If no sweep occurs, the next card is flipped and added to the pile.

**Extra Rules for Maximum Mayhem:**

- **Double Sweep:** If three or more people flip the same value, all must race to slap the pile. The last gets a double penalty—two drinks or a wild dare.
- **Joker’s Wild (optional):** If a Joker appears, everyone drinks and the round resets.
- **Custom Rules:** Use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to add meme dares, election day drinking game twists, or wedding party games themes.

**Winning the Game:**

The game goes until someone runs out of cards. The last person standing must complete a mega dare chosen by the group or drink a full glass—your call!
