3. **Draw and Dare**
   - On your turn, draw a card (or digital prompt).
     - **Red Card:** Assign a drink to anyone.
     - **Black Card:** You take a drink and perform a meme dare (Time Keeper picks a meme, you have to act it out).
     - **Face Card:** Group dare—everyone joins in on a viral TikTok dance, last to nail it drinks.

4. **Time Warp Challenge**
