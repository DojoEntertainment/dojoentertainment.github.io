## How to Play WWE Smackdown Drinking Game

**The Rules:**

This party drinking game is all about reacting to the wildest moments, meme-worthy promos, and classic wrestling chaos. Each time something happens on Smackdown, everyone drinks according to the list below. Want to turn up the heat? Add dare challenges or use the [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to throw in your own NSFW dares.

### Basic Drinks

- **Every time a wrestler’s entrance music hits:** Sip
- **When the crowd chants something iconic (YES! YES! YES!):** Sip
- **Every time the announcers shout a wrestler’s nickname:** Sip
- **If someone gets thrown over the ropes:** 2 sips
- **If a wrestler does a finishing move:** 2 sips
- **When a manager interferes:** 2 sips

### Wild Card Moments

- **Someone gets hit with a chair or foreign object:** Finish your drink
- **A surprise return or debut:** Everyone does a shot
- **If a wrestler cuts a meme-worthy promo:** Make up a dare challenge for someone else
- **Unexpected alliance or betrayal:** The whole group drinks

### Dare Challenges

Want to kick things up? Use dare cards or jot down custom NSFW dares to draw at random during commercial breaks, or whenever there’s a controversial call. (Think: “Do your best wrestler walkout,” “Snap a meme selfie in your best fighting pose,” or “Call out a friend for a 1v1 Fortnite drinking game next round.”)
