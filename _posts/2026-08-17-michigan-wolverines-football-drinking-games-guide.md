## Game 1: Touchdown Toast

**How to Play**  
Simple and classic. Every time Michigan scores a touchdown, everyone raises their glass and takes a drink. But here’s the twist—after the first touchdown, the last person to toast has to do a dare challenge (think NSFW dares if you’re feeling spicy, or keep it tame for family vibes).

**Rules:**  
- Every touchdown: Everyone drinks.
- Last to toast: Pulls a dare from the pile or uses a meme card.
- Bonus: If Michigan gets a pick-six, everyone does a group cheers and finishes their drink.

## Game 2: Meme Drinking Game

Combine Michigan football memes with drinking rules for a wild ride. Before the game, everyone writes down classic Wolverines moments, inside jokes, or player memes on cards.

**How to Play**  
- When a meme-worthy moment happens (think Harbaugh’s khakis, a wild interception, or a “Go Blue” chant), the person who wrote that meme hands out two drinks.
- If the moment happens twice, double it up—four drinks, or create a house rule.
- Miss a meme? Take a shot.

## Game 3: Quarterback Calls

Every time the Michigan quarterback yells an audible or changes the play at the line of scrimmage, everyone takes a sip. This is perfect for fans who know their football—or anyone who just likes to shout along.

**Rules:**  
- QB audible: Sip.
- False start: Last person to notice takes a dare challenge.
- Touchdown called back by penalty: Everyone finishes their drinks.
