## How To Play NBA Drinking Games

Let’s set the scene: tip-off just happened, snacks are out, and everyone’s ready for dare challenges. Here’s how you can turn any NBA matchup into a **viral drinking game** that’ll have you hyped till the final buzzer.

### Basic Rules

1. **Assign Teams:**  
   Split the room into Team A and Team B. Everyone picks their fave NBA team or player to root for.

2. **Drinking Triggers:**  
   Every time one of these happens, everyone takes a sip or follows the dare:  
   - Slam dunk: Everyone drinks.  
   - Three-pointer: Last person to raise their cup drinks twice.  
   - Foul: Opposing team does a **dare challenge** (get creative or use **NSFW dares** for extra chaos).  
   - Free throw miss: Shooter’s fans drink.  
   - Timeout called: Group selfie, everyone drinks.  
   - Meme moment (crazy hair, wild fan, mascot dance): Make up a dare on the spot.

3. **Quarter Breaks:**  
   - Winner of the quarter assigns a dare to the losing team.  
   - Losing team must share their wildest NBA hot take (or down their drink if they can’t top the last one).

4. **Final Buzzer:**  
   - Winning team celebrates with a victory toast.  
   - Losing team must do the ultimate dare challenge—think TikTok-worthy or grab something from the Drinking Dojo dare pack.

### House Rules for Extra Spice

- **Fortnite Drinking Game Crossover:**  
  If your squad’s also into gaming, throw in a **Fortnite drinking game**-style “chug jug” round during halftime—everyone drinks till the beat drops.  
  
- **Wedding Party Games Twist:**  
  Watching NBA games at a wedding afterparty? Mix in **couples drinking games** by pairing up and doubling dares for each missed free throw.
