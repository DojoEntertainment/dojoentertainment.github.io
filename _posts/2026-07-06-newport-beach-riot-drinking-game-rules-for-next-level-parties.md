Forget boring party drinking games. Newport Beach Riot is about wild dares, viral drinking games, and meme-worthy moments. Ready to shake things up? Here’s how to play.

## What You Need

- A squad of 4 or more (the more, the mayhem-ier)  
- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzers, cocktails—dealer’s choice)  
- A deck of cards (for rule twists)  
- A phone for dare challenges and meme drinking games  
- A speaker for riotous tunes  
- Optional: props for NSFW dares or viral TikTok challenges  

## How To Play Newport Beach Riot

### 1. **Set The Scene**  
Turn your space into Newport Beach riot mode—think neon lights, beach towels, and a playlist that slaps. Everyone cracks open their first drink.

### 2. **Deal The Cards**  
Shuffle the deck and deal everyone 5 cards. Each card has a drinking challenge attached (see below).

### 3. **Riot Card Rules**

- **Hearts**: Dare Challenges  
  Draw a heart? Pick someone to do a wild dare (think meme-worthy or NSFW dares if your squad’s into it). If they refuse, they drink.

- **Diamonds**: Viral Drinking Games  
  When a diamond shows up, start a quick viral drinking game—like “Never Have I Ever,” “Flip Cup,” or “Fortnite Drinking Game” (last one standing wins).

- **Clubs**: Meme Drinking Games  
  The person who draws a club has to send a meme to the group chat. If it flops (no one laughs), they chug.

- **Spades**: Riot Challenges  
  Spades are riot mode. Everyone has to do a group dare—think “shotgun a beer,” “dance-off,” or “wedding party games” like limbo with drinks in hand.

### 4. **Riot Rounds**  
After each full round (everyone plays a card), the group votes on the wildest moment. The winner picks someone to finish their drink—or gives out a dare.

### 5. **The Final Blowout**  
Once the cards run out, everyone attempts the “Riot Gauntlet”—a mashup of three dares and one drinking challenge (make it as spicy or silly as your squad can handle).
