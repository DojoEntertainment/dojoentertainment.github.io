## The NCAA Football Drinking Game: Kickoff to Chaos

### What You’ll Need

- Your favorite drinks (beer, seltzers, cocktails, or non-alcoholic if you’re feeling chill)
- A TV or streaming device for the game
- Friends (the more, the rowdier)
- A printed or digital NCAA football schedule
- Optional: Dare cards or our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) for next-level challenges

### Basic Rules

Pick a game from the NCAA football schedule and gather your crew. Here’s how it works:

**Take a sip whenever:**
- The announcer says “defense” or “momentum”
- A team gets a first down
- There’s a commercial for pizza or beer

**Take two sips if:**
- There’s a turnover (interception or fumble)
- Someone in the group yells at the ref on TV
- The mascot shows up on screen

**Finish your drink if:**
- There’s a kickoff or punt return for a touchdown
- Your team wins in overtime
- The half-time show is an absolute disaster

### Dare Challenges and Meme Moments

Want to spice things up? Add some dare challenges and meme drinking games into the mix:

- **NSFW dares:** Loser of each quarter has to complete a dare drawn from a hat—think “send a cringy meme to your crush” or “do your best touchdown dance on video.”
- **Viral drinking games:** Create a TikTok challenge based on the wildest play of the game.
- **Meme drinking games:** Every time a meme-worthy moment happens (think mascot fails or epic fan reactions), the group votes on who has to take a shot.
