## How To Play the Weather Boston Drinking Game

Every weather condition brings a new twist. Here’s how to get started:

### 1. Pick Your Setting

- **Indoors:** Perfect for snow days, rainy afternoons, or when the wind is howling off the Charles.
- **Outdoors:** Bring it to a backyard BBQ, beach day, or Boston Common picnic.

### 2. Set Up Your Game

- Divide everyone into teams or play individually.
- Use your weather app to check the current Boston forecast.
- Each weather condition triggers a different set of rules, dares, or drinking challenges.

### 3. Follow These Core Rules

- **Sunny:** For every 10 degrees above 60°F (15°C), take one sip. Over 80°F (27°C)? Everyone does a dare challenge—think meme-worthy dance moves, viral TikTok impressions, or classic NSFW dares.
- **Rainy:** Every time it starts to drizzle, last person to yell “Red Sox rain delay!” drinks. If the forecast calls for thunderstorms, everyone finishes their drink or does a dare.
- **Snowy:** If flakes are falling, the person dressed most like a tourist (bonus if they’re actually wearing a Harvard hoodie) drinks. If a snowplow goes by, everyone drinks.
- **Windy:** For every gust over 15 mph (24 kph), pass your drink to the left. If someone’s hat blows off, take two drinks.
- **Foggy:** Every time someone says “wicked,” everyone else drinks. If no one says it for ten minutes, the whole group does a group dare.
- **Wildcard:** Use the custom drinking game builder to add in Boston-specific events (like spotting a duck boat or hearing a Boston accent on TV).

### 4. Dare Challenge Ideas

- “Recite your best Mark Wahlberg impression or drink.”
- “Share your wildest house party ideas or do 10 jumping jacks.”
- “Name three Boston sports teams—miss one and face a dare.”
- “Couples drinking games twist: One couple does a duet of ‘Sweet Caroline’—losers drink.”
