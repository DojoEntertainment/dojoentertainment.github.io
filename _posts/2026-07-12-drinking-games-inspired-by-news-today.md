## The News Today Drinking Game

Let’s face it, the news is never boring. Why not turn it into your next favorite drinking game? Here’s everything you need to play, plus a few spicy twists to keep the fun rolling.

### What You’ll Need

- A TV, laptop, or smartphone with access to a news channel or news site  
- Your favorite drinks (cocktails, beers, or mocktails—your dojo, your rules)  
- A group of friends (2+ players)  
- A custom drinking game builder (optional, but [Drinking Dojo](https://drinkingdojo.com) makes it epic)  
- Dare cards or slips of paper for dares  

### How To Play

1. **Tune In:** Everyone gathers around and picks a live news broadcast or a trending news video (bonus points for choosing something spicy, like election day coverage or viral moments).
2. **Set Your Triggers:** Before you start, agree on a list of common news elements. Example triggers:
    - “Breaking news” banner pops up
    - Reporter stumbles over words
    - A meme-worthy headline appears
    - Someone mentions a celebrity
    - Awkward technical glitch or background fail
3. **Take A Sip:** Every time a trigger happens, everyone takes a drink.
4. **Dare Challenges:** If the group misses a trigger and someone calls it out, the first person to notice picks a dare for another player (NSFW dares are fair game if the vibe is right).
5. **Wildcard Rounds:** Every 15 minutes, pause and draw a wildcard news-related challenge (e.g., “Give your best weather reporter impression” or “Make up a breaking story about someone in the group”).
6. **Game Over:** Play for a set number of stories, or until the news stops being funny (as if that ever happens).

### Make It Viral

Want to take it up a notch? Add some meme drinking games elements:
- If a story hits social media and you see your group’s favorite meme referenced, everyone takes two drinks.
- Share the best meme-worthy moment of the night on your group chat (or even post it, if you dare).

### Party Variations

- **Election Day Drinking Game:** Pick triggers based on candidate buzzwords or wild debate moments.
- **Inauguration Day Drinking Game:** Drink for every awkward handshake or outfit fail.
- **Wedding Party Games:** Have everyone make up wild news stories about the couple.
- **Couples Drinking Games:** Teams of two compete for spotting the most triggers—losers do double dares.

### Pro Tips For Next-Level Fun

- Use a custom drinking game builder to set your own triggers and dares, themed to whatever crazy story is trending today.
- Mix in physical challenges and meme drinking games for the ultimate party experience.
- Always keep it safe and know your limits—remember, the best stories are the ones you remember!
