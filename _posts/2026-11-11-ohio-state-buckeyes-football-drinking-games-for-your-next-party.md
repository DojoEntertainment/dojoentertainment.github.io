Set these up before the game, and add your own wild spins with custom dares or viral drinking games from Drinking Dojo.

### Take a Sip When

- The Buckeyes score a touchdown  
- The other team gets a first down  
- The announcer mentions a Heisman Trophy  
- The camera cuts to Brutus Buckeye (mascot alert!)  
- Someone in the room yells “O-H!” (bonus: everyone else must reply “I-O!” or take a drink)

### Take Two Sips If

- There’s a turnover  
- A coach is shown losing their mind on the sidelines  
- The refs throw a flag for anything  
- The marching band busts out a meme-worthy formation

### Finish Your Drink If

- The Buckeyes win by more than 20 points  
- The game goes into overtime  
- Someone in the room successfully predicts a play (before the snap—no cheaters)

### Dare Challenges (Optional for the Brave)

- If you spill your drink, pull an NSFW dare  
- During commercials, launch a mini meme drinking game: each player shares a Buckeyes meme; best one picks who drinks  
- Loser of a halftime coin flip does a dare challenge (think viral TikTok dares or embarrassing Buckeye chants)
