#### The Main Event: Marathon Triggers

Take a sip, shot, or complete a dare when any of these marathon moments happen:

- **Runner in costume** passes by
- **Someone stops for water**
- **Spectator sign** that makes you laugh
- **TV commentator mentions the weather**
- **Race leader changes**
- **Someone trips but keeps running**

Bonus: If you spot a couple running together, cue up a couples drinking game round!

#### Dare Challenges

Draw a card or spin the dare wheel if:

- You see a viral moment (someone does a backflip, proposes, or the crowd goes wild)
- Your predicted winner drops out
- A runner high-fives a spectator
- You hear a meme-worthy quote from the broadcast
