## Game 1: Wimbledon Rally Royale

**Materials Needed:**
- Drinks (beer, cider, seltzers—your choice)
- TV or livestream of Wimbledon today
- Scorecards (optional for extra meme drinking games drama)

**How To Play:**
1. **Choose Players:** Split into two teams or play solo.
2. **Serve Up:** Every time the server double faults, everyone drinks.
3. **Game Points:** If the game goes to deuce, last person to shout “Wimbledon!” drinks twice.
4. **Ace Action:** When a player serves an ace, pick someone to do a dare challenge (think NSFW dares if the party’s wild).
5. **Rally Counter:** For every rally over 10 shots, count out loud—whoever loses track drinks.
6. **Winning Shot:** When a player wins a set, everyone on the losing side drinks.

**House Party Twist:** Add a custom drinking game builder rule—like drink every time you hear “out!” or a commentator makes a meme-worthy pun.

## Game 2: Strawberries And Sips

Celebrate Wimbledon’s iconic snack with this viral drinking game.

**Materials Needed:**
- Strawberries
- Cream (or whipped cream)
- Your favorite drink

**How To Play:**
1. **Snack Attack:** Each time the camera cuts to someone eating strawberries and cream, everybody takes a sip.
2. **Dare Time:** If a player on screen eats something else (banana, energy gel, whatever), the group nominates someone for a dare challenge.
3. **Final Set Showdown:** For the last set of the match, swap your drink for a strawberry shot (slice a strawberry, dip in cream, add a shot of your drink).

## Game 3: Meme The Match

Perfect for meme drinking games fans and anyone who loves a bit of chaos.

**Materials Needed:**
- Phones for meme searching or meme-making
- Drinks
- Wimbledon livestream

**How To Play:**
1. **Meme Spotting:** Whenever a commentator says something meme-worthy, the first person to find a related meme online assigns drinks.
2. **Outfit Awards:** If you spot wild tennis fashion (think headbands or neon shoes), everyone votes on the best look. Losers drink.
3. **Crowd Cutaway:** When the camera shows a famous spectator, last to name them drinks. If nobody knows, everyone drinks.
