## Easy Formula 1 Drinking Games To Start With

### Classic F1 Sip and Go

**How to Play:**
1. Everyone picks a favorite driver or team before the race.
2. Take a sip every time your driver is mentioned on the broadcast.
3. Take two sips if your driver overtakes another racer.
4. Finish your drink if your driver crashes out (ouch).
5. If someone’s pick gets a podium, they assign a dare challenge to another player.

### Meme Drinking Game

Formula 1 is basically a meme goldmine. Turn every viral moment into a party drinking game:

- Drink every time the commentators mention tires or strategy.
- Chug if someone posts a meme-worthy radio message.
- Dare a friend if the camera cuts to an awkward celebrity in the paddock.

### Dare Pit Stop

Spice things up with NSFW dares or wedding party games (for those wild bachelor weekends):

- On every safety car, the last person to touch their drink must draw a dare card.
- If there’s a red flag, it’s time for a group challenge—think viral drinking games or custom dares from our builder.
