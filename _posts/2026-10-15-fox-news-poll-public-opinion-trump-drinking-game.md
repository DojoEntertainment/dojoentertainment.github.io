## How To Play: Fox News Poll Public Opinion Trump Drinking Game

This party classic keeps things fresh, unpredictable, and totally meme drinking games style. Here’s how to get started:

### 1. Set Up Your Dojo

Gather everyone in the living room or around a big screen. Make sure everyone’s got a drink in hand and is ready for anything.

### 2. Pick Your Host

Nominate a “Game Master” to keep things moving and call out the drinking cues. Bonus points if they do their best news anchor impression.

### 3. Choose Your Fox News Poll Segment

Stream the latest coverage or scroll through recent poll updates about Trump. Trust us, there’s always something new. If you’re feeling creative, use our [custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to personalize your rules to match the latest headlines.

### 4. The Drinking Rules

Take a sip, a shot, or complete a dare every time:

- **Trump’s approval rating is mentioned**  
  *Take a drink*
- **A poll result surprises the panel**  
  *Everyone cheers and drinks*
- **Anyone says “unprecedented,” “shocking,” or “historic”**  
  *Last person to raise their glass does a dare challenge*
- **A meme-worthy comment is made**  
  *Vote for the best meme in the group chat; loser takes two drinks*
- **A guest starts a political rant**  
  *Interrupt with a toast and make them do an NSFW dare (keep it friendly and safe!)*

### Bonus Round: Debate Showdown

Pause the news and split into two teams for a quick-fire debate on a ridiculous poll question (think: “Should pineapple go on pizza?”). The winning team picks any player to finish their drink or complete a wild dare.
