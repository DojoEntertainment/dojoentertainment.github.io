## Classic NFL Drinking Game Rules to Boost Your Party Drinking Games

### 1. **First Down Sips**
Every time your team gets a first down, everyone on your side takes a sip. If the *other* team gets a first down, do a dare challenge from the group.

### 2. **Touchdown Toast**
When your team scores a touchdown, everyone does a celebratory shot. Bonus: The person who predicted the exact scoring play gets to nominate someone for an NSFW dare (keep it friendly!).

### 3. **Flag On The Play**
Penalty? Everyone takes a drink. If it’s a personal foul, the last person to shout “No chill!” finishes their drink.

### 4. **Commercial Meme Break**
During every commercial break, someone picks a meme from their phone. If it gets a laugh, they hand out two drinks. If it flops, they take a drink themselves.

## Dare Challenges To Keep Things Wild During Your NFL Party

Looking for viral drinking games energy? Here are some NFL-themed dare challenges for your group:

- **End Zone Dance-Off:** Lost a bet? Show off your best touchdown dance or drink twice.
- **Mascot Mayhem:** Draw a random NFL mascot. If no one guesses it, you do a dare.
- **Coach’s Challenge:** If a play goes to review, everyone guesses the outcome — losers take a shot.
