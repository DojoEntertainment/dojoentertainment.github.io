## How To Play: Lose Yourself Drinking Game

This party drinking game is simple, wild, and perfect for fans from Detroit to Denmark.

### Basic Rules

1. **Pick Your Playlist:** Queue up a playlist of Eminem classics—think “Lose Yourself,” “Without Me,” “Love the Way You Lie,” and more.  
2. **Set Your Triggers:** Every player draws a card (or use your phone and a meme drinking games app) to get a trigger word, phrase, or dare challenge.  
3. **Drink When...**  
   - Eminem mentions his mom, daughter, or Detroit  
   - He says “Slim Shady” or “real”  
   - There’s a celebrity roast or meme-worthy lyric  
   - The beat drops hard  
   - Anyone in the room fails to keep up with the lyrics (bonus: NSFW dares for the lyric fumble)  
4. **Dare Challenges:** At random intervals, pause the music and hit a meme drinking game dare—like rapping a verse, impersonating Em’s iconic hoodie shuffle, or sending a meme to the group chat.  
5. **Couples Twist:** If you’re playing as pairs, couples drinking games rules apply—swap drinks if your partner misses a lyric or gets roasted by the group.
