## How to Play Meteor Shower

Ready for a party drinking game that’s all about energy and fast moves? Here’s how Meteor Shower works:

### Setup

1. **Deal cards face down** in a circle, one per player.
2. Give each player a drink and a meteor token.
3. Place a stack of dare challenges in the middle (or load up on wild NSFW dares from Drinking Dojo if you’re feeling spicy).

### Rules

- When the round starts, set a 2-minute timer — the "shower" is about to begin.
- On “GO,” every player flips their card. If you get a **number card**, you’re safe. If you get a **face card** (J, Q, K, Ace), you become a “Meteor.”
- Meteors must toss their token to another player *across* the circle.
- If you catch a meteor token, you have 5 seconds to pass it to someone else who doesn’t have one, or you drink.
- If you drop your meteor token or hold it too long, you do a dare challenge.
- When the timer ends, anyone still holding a meteor token has to finish their drink and do a meme-worthy dare.

### Extra Chaos

- Add a “shooting star” round: everyone stands and spins once before tossing tokens for extra mayhem.
- Mix in viral drinking games or Fortnite drinking game rules for crossover chaos (try “build a fort” with coasters as a silly sideline).
