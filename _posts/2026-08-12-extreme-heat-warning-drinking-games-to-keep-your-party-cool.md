## 1. Sunburn or Sips: The Outdoor Dare Challenge

**What you need:**  
- A deck of cards  
- Plenty of sunscreen  
- Chilled drinks  
- A shady spot (trust us)

**How to play:**  
- Everyone sits in a circle, drinks in hand.  
- Take turns drawing a card. Each card equals a dare challenge, with a heat-themed twist—think “apply sunscreen to someone else’s nose” or “hold an ice cube until it melts.”  
- Red cards? Take a sip. Black cards? Assign a dare.  
- Jokers call for a group dare—like running around the yard with your shoes on your hands or inventing an instant meme drinking game on the spot.

**Why it works:**  
It keeps you laughing, moving, and way less likely to pass out in the sun. Plus, sunscreen reapplication is never a bad idea.

## 2. The Ice Bucket Drinking Challenge

**What you need:**  
- Buckets of ice  
- Cups  
- A big table  
- Your favorite drinks

**How to play:**  
- Fill cups with ice and drinks.  
- Players compete in silly challenges (think “hold your hand in the ice bucket longest” or “guess the meme on the card”).  
- Winners assign drinks, losers take the dare challenge: a quick ice dump over the head or a round of NSFW dares (for the brave).

**Why it works:**  
It’s the ultimate way to cool down fast, and it’s always hilarious to watch your mates take the plunge.

## 4. Couples Drinking Games for Steamy Nights

Extreme heat warning? Turn it up with intimate, hilarious couples drinking games.

**What you need:**  
- A couples dare pack (or use the custom builder)  
- Cold cocktails  
- Blankets for indoor picnics (or moonlit garden hangs)

**How to play:**  
- Take turns drawing cards with dares, meme challenges, or questions.  
- Loser takes a drink or performs a dare—think “share your most embarrassing sunburn story” or “reenact a viral meme with your partner.”  
- First to back out owes the other a frozen drink.

**Why it works:**  
Perfect for date nights or double dates when the AC’s cranked and you want to keep things hot (but not too hot).
