- **Try, Goal, or Wicket:**  
  Everyone on the *opposite* team takes a drink.
- **Epic Fail (missed catch, fumble, major meme moment):**  
  Last person to react does a dare challenge.
- **Ref Decision Causes Chaos:**  
  Everyone drinks.
- **Player Injury Timeout:**  
  Everyone must freeze. Last to freeze drinks.
- **Commentator Drops a Classic Aussie or South African Slang:**  
  First to shout out the meaning assigns two drinks.
- **Streaker, Pitch Invasion, or Mascot Antics:**  
  All-in dare challenge, picked by the referee.
