## Top Drinking Games for Constitution Day 2026

### 1. **Constitutional Chaos**

A custom drinking game builder classic—perfect for mixing history with hilarity.

**Materials:**
- Deck of cards
- A list of funny (and maybe a little NSFW) dares  
- Drinks of choice

**How to Play:**
- Assign each card a rule (e.g., Ace = Give a speech, King = Make a new “amendment” for the party, Queen = Dare challenge).
- When someone draws a card, they act out the rule.
- Bonus: Use the [Drinking Dojo custom drinking game builder](https://drinkingdojo.com) to create your own wild rules and meme dares!

### 2. **Founding Fathers’ Dare Challenge**

Bring some viral drinking games energy to your party with this crowd favorite.

**Materials:**
- Slip of paper or an app with dare challenges (think: dramatic readings, impromptu “laws”, NSFW dares for the brave)
- Drinks

**How to Play:**
- Everyone draws a dare. Complete it or drink.
- Extra points if you do your dare in an over-the-top statesman voice.

### 3. **Red White and Boozed**

Perfect for big groups and house parties—this one gets everyone moving.

**Materials:**
- Three cups (red, white, blue if you’re feeling festive)
- Ping pong balls
- Drinks

**How to Play:**
- Split into teams.
- Try to bounce a ball into the cups. Each color equals a different challenge or drinking rule (e.g., red = meme drinking games round, blue = couples drinking games challenge).
- First team to clear their cups wins bragging rights and maybe a round of not drinking.

### 4. **Election Day Throwback**

Why not bring some election day drinking game energy to Constitution Day 2026?

**Materials:**
- List of “campaign promises” (funny dares or challenges)
- Drinks

**How to Play:**
- Everyone makes a “campaign promise” at the start of the night.
- If you break your promise (forget to use your “presidential” accent, or miss a rule), you drink.
