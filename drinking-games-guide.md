# Which Drinking Game Should You Play?

Choose by the people in the room, the equipment available, and how much conversation or competition your group wants. Drinking Dojo's browser-based games are designed for adults of legal drinking age. Alcohol is optional: water or another non-alcoholic drink works with the same prompts and rules.

## Quick comparison

| Game | Best fit | Equipment | Play style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Truth or Dare](https://drinkingdojo.com/games/truth-or-dare) | Friends who enjoy prompts and storytelling | One connected screen | Conversational |

| [Drunk Dice](https://drinkingdojo.com/games/drunk-dice) | Casual groups wanting random challenges | A device or one die | Chance-based |

## Use cases

### For a remote game night

Pick a prompt-based game that does not rely on objects moving between players. Truth or Dare, Never Have I Ever, and Most Likely To can all be coordinated over a video call.

### For new groups

Start with low-stakes Most Likely To prompts. Agree that anyone can skip a prompt, and avoid questions about private or sensitive topics.

### For a competitive group

Meyer adds bluffing and turn-taking. Drunk Dice is a simpler alternative when the group wants outcomes based on chance.

### For an alcohol-free party

Use the same game rules with non-alcoholic drinks, points, or tokens. The social format does not require alcohol.
